# Demo video for App Review (iOS)

App Review rejected iOS 1.0 (3) on 2026-09-21 under 2.1, Information Needed,
and asked for a video of the app on a physical device. Record on 1.0 (5),
which is the build that answers them. The reviewer, on an
iPad Pro 11-inch (M4), stopped at "Undirect is not on" (their screenshot).
The video has to show every step from that screen to a stopped pop.

Record on an iPad if you have one, since that is what they review on;
otherwise an iPhone. Turn on Screen Recording in Control Center, and keep
notifications off (Do Not Disturb). Aim for two to three minutes, no cuts.

Before recording, switch Undirect off in Settings, then Apps, then Safari,
then Extensions, so the video starts where the reviewer did.

## Shots

1. Open TestFlight on Undirect so the version and build number are on screen,
   then press Open.
2. The app shows "Turn on Undirect in Safari". Press Open Safari Extensions.
3. Settings opens on Undirect's page under Safari Extensions. Turn it on. If
   it opens somewhere else, go to Apps, then Safari, then Extensions, then
   Undirect.
4. On the same screen, set All Websites to Allow, and hold on that screen
   long enough to read its permission text.
5. Switch back to Undirect. The setup screen is gone and the rules list shows.
6. Open Safari and go to undirect.matsuokengo.com/demo/.
7. Tap anywhere on the page. With Undirect off for this site, a new tab opens
   on a made-up address. Close it.
8. Press the page menu at the left of the address bar, then Undirect. The
   popup says the guard is off here. Turn the switch on; the page reloads.
9. Tap the page again. Nothing opens. Press the page menu, then Undirect: the
   popup lists each address it stopped, marked Blocked.
10. Change one row's Blocked to Allowed, then back to Blocked.
11. In the popup, open Rules, go back, then open Settings.
12. Switch to the Undirect app. The list now shows the same addresses under
    Everywhere, and the demo site under Guarded sites.

Upload it as an unlisted video, or give the file to Claude to host on
