One X reply-picks sitting for @monholtco (card ASG212, started by launchd through Support/social/reply-sitting.sh x).

Goal: the owner writes @monholtco's replies himself, in his own words, so no reply on X is AI-written. This sitting finds the posts worth replying to and gives him what he needs to write a good reply fast. It never posts, replies, likes or follows on X.

In Claude in Chrome, signed in as @monholtco, reading only:
- Check the account is in good standing (notifications for any removed post, label or lock); if anything is there, say so in the PushNotification.
- Find 3 posts under about 2 hours old from indie Apple, Mac and iOS makers, app directories that list our apps, and people with the pains Undirect and Cutling fix (search Latest, the home feed, the accounts @monholtco follows). Skip engagement farms, "drop your link" threads and posts that already have dozens of replies.
- Pick only a post that leaves the owner something real to say: it asks a question, shares a number or result he can ask about, names a problem or decision he has faced with his own apps (App Review, pricing, launch channels, Safari, keyboards, clipboard, localisation), or makes a claim he can add his own experience to. A plain launch or feature announcement ("X is here", a screenshot and a tagline) leaves nothing but "congrats" or a feature question, so skip it. Fewer picks are better than a weak one: hand in 0-3, and for each say in one line why it gives him something to say.
- Read everything each post carries: its link, video, image and the replies already under it.
- Run `bun ~/Repositories/monholt/hq/scripts/picks.ts list` first and leave out any post already listed there.
- Hand the posts in to Monholt HQ, where the owner reads them on his phone with the Mac off: write a JSON list to a file in $TMPDIR and run `bun ~/Repositories/monholt/hq/scripts/picks.ts add <file>`. Each item: `account` ("x"), `url` (the post's own link), `author` (@handle), `who` (followers and what they make), `gist` (what the post says, one line), `text` (the post's words), `postedAt` (ISO time), `context` (what its link, video or image shows and what the replies already say), `angles` (2-3 notes he could take: a question only a maker would ask, a fact from his own apps he could add, what not to say because someone already said it; notes, never ready-to-paste text). If the script refuses an item, fix that item and run it again.
- If a post idea of the owner's own kind came up (an announcement of what he made or shipped, in "I", with his own numbers; no forced question, no ad), hand in at most one with `picks.ts idea <file>`: `text`, `why` (one line), `accounts`.
- Send the owner a PushNotification: "<n> X posts worth a reply on hq.monholt.com/picks", with the age of the freshest one.
- If Chrome shows no connected browser or X is signed out, send the owner a PushNotification saying so and stop.
