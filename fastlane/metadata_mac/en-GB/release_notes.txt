On a site where Undirect is on, a page that tries to send you to another site without a tap, as it loads, after a delay or while you are just scrolling, now stays put. The address it tried appears in Undirect's list, so you can allow it if you wanted to go there.
Back works again on those pages. A page that answers Back by sending you elsewhere stays where it is, and a page that stacks copies of itself in your history so Back never gets you out now keeps just one.
