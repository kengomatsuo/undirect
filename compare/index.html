Safari Block Pop-ups, content blockers and Undirect compared

Three different tools get called a pop-up blocker. Undirect is not an ad blocker. Facts below come from Apple's support pages and each product's own App Store listing, read on 7 November 2026 in the US store.

Where the others do more

1Blocker and AdGuard block ads, trackers and page clutter, which Undirect does not touch. If banners, video ads or tracking are what bother you, use one of them. Safari's own switch costs nothing and covers pop-up windows, so there is no reason to leave it off.

What Undirect does not do

It does not hide ads, banners or cookie notices, and it does not block trackers. It works in Safari only, on the sites where you have switched it on, and it needs iOS 26.2 or later. It also costs money where the others have a free tier. You can run a content blocker beside it, though we have not tested every combination.

Where it fits

Undirect is for the page that takes your tap: the layer you cannot see, the link that changes under your finger, the tab you did not open. For that, read how to stop Safari redirects and pop-ups on iPhone, or try the demo sites before you buy.
