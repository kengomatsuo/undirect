## Common problems

**Undirect does not appear in Safari**

Open the app once, then switch Undirect on in Safari's extension settings.

**How do I turn it on for a site?**

Open the site in Safari and press the Undirect button. The guard is off everywhere until you do. Press it again to turn it off for that site.

**A site stopped working after I set a rule**

Press the extension button, find the host and choose its default option. That removes the rule.

**I cannot change rules on iPhone or iPad**

The list in the app is read only there, because iOS gives an app no way to send changes to its own extension. Change rules from the extension button in Safari.

**A window I wanted was blocked**

Its host is in the list. Allow it on that site, or everywhere.
