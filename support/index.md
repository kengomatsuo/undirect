## Common problems

**Undirect does not appear in Safari**

Open the app once, then switch Undirect on in Safari's extension settings.

**How do I turn it on for a site?**

Open the site in Safari and press the Undirect button. The guard is off everywhere until you do. Press it again to turn it off for that site.

**A site stopped working after I set a rule**

Press the extension button, find the host and choose its default option. That removes the rule.

**I cannot change rules on iPhone or iPad**

The list in the app is read only there, because iOS gives an app no way to send changes to its own extension. Change rules from the extension button in Safari.

**A window I wanted was blocked**

Its host is in the list. Allow it on that site, or everywhere.

**Why am I still getting pop-ups on Safari?**

Safari's Block Pop-ups only stops windows a page opens without a tap. Most ad pop-ups wait for your tap, so Safari lets them through. Undirect checks whether the tab is what you pressed and stops the ones that are not.

**Is my iPhone hacked or infected?**

Almost always no. A “virus found” or “iPhone at risk” page is an ad the website sent you to. Close the tab and do not install or pay for anything it asks. Undirect stops these when a tap set them off.

**Does it stop redirects that happen without me tapping?**

Yes, from version 1.0.5. On a site where Undirect is on, a page that tries to send you to another site with no tap (on load, on a timer or while you scroll) stays put, and the address shows in Undirect's list so you can allow it if you want.

**Do I still need an ad blocker?**

Undirect does not hide ads on the page. It stops the tabs and redirects that ad blockers miss. Many people run both.

**Is it a subscription?**

No. You pay once and it works on your iPhone, iPad and Mac.
