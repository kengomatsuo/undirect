Lemon barley soup that tastes like more effort than it was

By Ines Varga · 40 minutes · serves four

Pearl barley turns the broth silky while the lemon keeps it bright. Everything goes in one pot, and it is better on the second day.

You will need

  • 200 g pearl barley
  • 1 litre chicken or vegetable stock
  • 2 carrots, 1 leek, 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 lemon, zest and juice

Method

Soften the leek and carrot in oil. Add the garlic, barley and stock, then simmer for 30 minutes. Stir in the lemon at the end.
