## Undirect

- Name: Undirect
- Website: https://undirect.matsuokengo.com/
- Tagline (53): Safari extension that stops redirects and pop-up tabs
- Short (130): A Safari extension for Mac, iPhone and iPad that stops pages taking your click to open tabs or send you somewhere you didn't ask for.
- Categories: Productivity, Privacy/Security, Browser extensions, Utilities
- Price: $1.99 once (Black Friday $0.99, Nov 27 to 30)
- Source: https://github.com/kengomatsuo/undirect

Description:

Some pages lay an invisible layer over themselves, and your next click opens an ad tab instead of the link you aimed at. Others rewrite a link between your press and the release, or open a blank tab and quietly send the page you were reading somewhere else. Undirect is a Safari extension for Mac, iPhone and iPad that clears the layer before your press lands and puts a rewritten link back, so you end up where you meant to go.

Every place a page tried to send you shows up in the toolbar popup, stopped or not. You can allow or block each one on that site alone or everywhere, and whatever you block everywhere becomes a Safari content blocking rule, so the request never leaves your device.

- Guards only the sites you turn it on for, with one press of its toolbar button
- No account, no analytics, no server
- Open source under GPL-3.0
- $1.99 once on the App Store, Mac and iPhone/iPad in one purchase

## Cutling

- Name: Cutling
- Website: https://cutling.matsuokengo.com/
- Tagline (56): Clipboard keyboard for the text you type again and again
- Short (139): Keep your address, bank details, signature and canned replies in Cutling, then paste them with one tap from its keyboard or your Mac menu bar.
- Categories: Productivity, Utilities, Keyboards, Menu bar
- Price: $4.99 once (Black Friday $2.99, Nov 27 to 30)
- Source: https://github.com/kengomatsuo/Cutling

Description:

Cutling keeps the text you type over and over, like your address, bank details, an email signature or a reply you send every week, and pastes it with one tap. On iPhone and iPad it is a keyboard you switch to in any app. On the Mac it lives in the menu bar, and Shift-Command-V opens a picker right at your cursor, wherever you're typing.

The Mac app also keeps a history of what you copy, so you can save the ones worth keeping with a click. Anything a password manager marks as concealed is left out of it.

- Up to 100 text snippets and 25 images, each with its own colour and icon
- Snippets can expire on a date you pick, for one-time codes and temporary details
- iCloud sync between iPhone, iPad and Mac, if you turn it on
- No account, no ads, no tracking
- Open source under GPL-3.0
- $4.99 once on the App Store, Mac and iPhone/iPad in one purchase
