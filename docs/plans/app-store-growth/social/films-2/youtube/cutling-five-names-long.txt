Five customers asked the same question overnight: has my order shipped? I answered all five with one saved reply that has a blank for the name. Tap the reply, tap the name, send.

That's the fill-in blank in Cutling 2.0, a keyboard for the text you type again and again, on iPhone, iPad and Mac.
