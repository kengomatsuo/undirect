# Pain posts, Sun Nov 22 to Sun Dec 20 2026, three a day (cards ASG73, ASG98)

@monholtco posts three times a day on X, Instagram and TikTok (@monholt), for two apps: Undirect (Safari pop-up and redirect blocker for iPhone, iPad and Mac, $1.99 once) and Cutling (clipboard and saved-text keyboard for iPhone and iPad, with full clipboard history on Mac, $4.99 once). 87 slots over 29 days. Nothing goes out without the owner's go, given by row number to the session that posts (asg-social-2).

Each post starts from something buyers say, ranked in `docs/research/undirect-voice-of-customer.md` (U#) and `docs/research/cutling-voice-of-customer.md` (C#, with the extra rows in `docs/research/cutling-niche-language.md`). One app per post. The only rows that name both apps are the two KEPT Black Friday rows.

## Times

A day is the WIB date. Each day has three slots, written in WIB with the US Eastern time beside it (Eastern is on standard time all through the run, so WIB is always 12 hours ahead):

| Slot | WIB | Eastern | US part of day |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 02:20 | 14:20 the day before | afternoon |
| 2 | 05:50 | 17:50 the day before | evening |
| 3 | 21:20 | 09:20 the same day | morning |

Source: `docs/research/social-formats-2026.md` has no posting times, so these come from two published best-time studies. Both report the viewer's local time, not one zone, so I read them as a US audience's local time and chose Eastern. A reader on the Pacific coast sees the slots three hours earlier.
- Buffer, "The Best Time to Post on Social Media in 2026" (published Mar 25 2026, 52M+ posts): X 8-11 a.m. (best 9 a.m. Tuesday), Instagram 9 a.m. and 6 p.m., TikTok 6-11 p.m. https://buffer.com/resources/best-time-to-post-on-social-media/ , read 2026-11-21.
- Sprout Social, "Best Times to Post on Social Media 2026" (published Mar 31 2026, about 2 billion engagements, data Nov 27 2025 to Feb 27 2026): X Tuesday to Thursday 12-6 p.m., Instagram Tuesday 1-7 p.m. and Wednesday 12-9 p.m., TikTok Tuesday to Friday 2-6 p.m. https://sproutsocial.com/insights/best-times-to-post-on-social-media/ , read 2026-11-21.

Slot 3 (09:20) is Buffer's morning window for X and Instagram. Slot 1 (14:20) sits inside Sprout's window for all three platforms. Slot 2 (17:50) sits at the end of Sprout's X, Instagram and TikTok windows and just before Buffer's 6 p.m. Instagram and TikTok windows. The two studies disagree on X (Buffer says morning, Sprout says midday to late afternoon), so the slots cover both. Sprout puts the weakest Instagram days on weekends, and the weekend slots stay because the owner asked for every day.

None of the slots is at 07:13, 10:07 or 15:07 WIB, where the listing posts sit. The four KEPT rows keep their own days and times and each takes the place of the nearest slot that day, so every day still has three.

## How each platform gets it

- X: the text as written, no hashtags. The link goes in our own first reply ("X reply" in each row), since free accounts lose reach on link posts (Buffer, Oct 2 2025). Polls run one day, 2-4 options of 25 characters at most.
- Instagram: Reel, image or carousel with the caption as written, "Link in bio.", then at most 5 tags. The pain sits in the first 125 characters. Story rows use the sticker named in the row, and the next Story frame carries a link sticker.
- TikTok: the caption as written, then "Search Undirect (or Cutling) on the App Store.", since TikTok links do not click, then 3-5 tags. The search phrase is also said and shown in the video. Never use #safaritips: on TikTok it is zoo travel tips.
- Tags, from `social-formats-2026.md`: Undirect Instagram `#iphonetips #iphonehacks #safari #adblock #iosapps`, TikTok `#iphonetips #iphonehacks #popupads #techtok`. Cutling Instagram `#iphonetips #textreplacement #productivityapps #iosapps #macapps`, TikTok `#textreplacement #iphonetips #iphonehacks #techtok`. Mac rows swap in the Mac sets and the Instagram-seller row the small-business set, as the captions below show.
- Interactive formats: X polls (13 rows), Instagram Story stickers (poll, quiz, emoji slider, questions, Add Yours) and TikTok poll stickers, comment polls and Q&A, all in the poll rows.

## Rules this file keeps

- Cutling does not lead with bank details and says nothing about an iPhone clipboard history. On iPhone it keeps what you save. The Mac app keeps the full history and skips passwords your manager marks as concealed. Posts that say the history is full say "Mac".
- Fill-ins posts are WITHDRAWN (the old P21 and CI in `pains-rewrite.md`) until cutling-pains sends the redesigned clip (ASG84-ASG91). None is in this calendar.
- Rows marked AFTER 1.0.5 (P41, P45, P52, P70, P82, plus u-no-tap and u-back wherever they appear) wait until `python3 Support/AppStore/asc_state.py 1.0.5` prints IOS READY_FOR_SALE. If it is not live on the day, swap the row with the nearest later Undirect row that is not marked, and post that one now.
- Nov 27 to Dec 1 (until 15:00 WIB on Dec 1, the end of Nov 30 Pacific) the Black Friday prices are on. Rows in that window carry no price and no film with a price end card. Their replies drop the price.
- Never post a text twice. If a row moves, rewrite its first line.
