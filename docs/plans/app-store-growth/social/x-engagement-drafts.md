# @monholtco on X, Nov 23 to Dec 6: fourteen posts written for replies

Drafts only, written 2026-11-22. Nothing here is scheduled, posted or committed. The research and
every count behind it is `docs/research/x-engagement-mix.md` (14 accounts, 398 posts, read on X that day).

## What was wrong with the calendar

`calendar-final/x-posts.ts` holds 84 English posts. 82 of the 84 name Cutling or Undirect and 83 end on
a link. Its entries for P03, P15 and P24 also replace three polls that `calendar-final.txt` had written
with no pitch ("Ad tabs you closed last night to watch one episode: 0 / 1-5 / 6-20 / Lost count") with
a product line and a link, so even the polls sell. The accounts read for the research
put their product's link in 18% of posts (makers) and 8% (brands), on launch, release and sale days
only, and a new brand account that only announces (@macpiphq, opened this month like ours) is seen by
a median of 7 people a post.

Questions and polls run above the researched share on purpose: they are what the owner asked for and
what drew replies on the smallest accounts read (@rashem48 at 2K, @iPritamX at 1.4K). A native poll
on an account with almost no followers will draw few votes (the four polls read drew votes from 0.25%
to 0.7% of followers), so only two are native and the third is written as text, where every answer
is a reply.

No poll or question names or points at either app, and no reply under one carries a link.

## Times

Each post has its own time in WIB, on odd minutes, at a different hour from the day before; the gaps
between posts run from 7 to 39 hours. Nobody's follower activity can be seen from outside (X shows it
only to the account itself), so the hours come from the next best thing that could be read: the
minute each of the 35 most replied-to posts in the research went out. 26 of the 35 fell between 15:00
and 00:15 WIB (Europe's morning through the US morning) and 8 between 01:45 and 07:00 WIB (the US
afternoon and evening). Twelve posts sit in the first band and two early-morning ones (Nov 29, Dec 5)
catch the US evening before. WIB is US Eastern plus 12 hours and Central European plus 6.

The Black Friday post is at 23:26 because the Arabic thank-you quote is already scheduled for
Nov 27 at 20:15 WIB and originals stay three hours apart.
