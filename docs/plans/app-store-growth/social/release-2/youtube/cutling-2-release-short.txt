Cutling 2.0 is out. Five things changed:

1. A saved reply can ask for the name before it types itself.
2. Dates fill in on the day you send them.
3. You can write a reply like a note, with bold, highlight and lists.
4. There's room for 5,000 texts and 500 images, up from 100 and 25.
5. Every key types, formatted ones too.

Cutling is a keyboard for the text you type again and again, on iPhone, iPad and Mac. Which of the five would you use first? Tell me in the comments.
