App Store conversion · Cutling and Undirect · 18 Nov 2026

The price is what stops the download

Undirect gave away 411 copies in 22 free days and has sold 9 in the 17 paid days since, with seven times the impressions. Apple's own peer numbers show the same gap for every Utilities app. Three page fixes follow, but none of them moves the number the way the price does. The price model is your call.

The ways to change the price

  • Free download, one-time unlock inside the appWhat every big keyboard and blocker does. Needs a StoreKit purchase and a locked tier built into each app, then App Review. Keeps the revenue from the people who like it.
  • A free week now and thenRepeats what Oct 10 did: deals accounts post free apps and the downloads bring ratings. No build needed; both price changes go into the schedule at once, so it switches back by itself.
  • Lower priceCutling $4.99 to $1.99 or $0.99. No build needed. Phraser, the cheapest snippet keyboard, unlocks for $0.99.
  • Free outrightNo build needed. Ends the revenue, which so far is $19 for Undirect since launch and $18 for Cutling since Aug 20.

Undirect · needs a build

About one iPhone in five cannot install Undirect

Undirect requires iOS 26.2. Apple counted 79% of all iPhones and 68% of all iPads on iOS 26 on June 7, 2026, so the rest see "Requires iOS 26.2" where the price button should be. Every comparable blocker in the market read runs on iOS 18.5 or older, most on iOS 15.

The floor was raised on Sep 22 for one thing: the setup button that opens Undirect's own page in Safari's settings, which only iOS 26.2 offers. I test-built the iOS app at iOS 18 today. The only failures are four calls in App/ExtensionStatus.swift, all part of that button. On older iOS the button would open the Settings app with the three steps written out instead. Not yet checked: the extension itself running in Safari 18, because no iOS 18 simulator is installed (about 8 GB).
