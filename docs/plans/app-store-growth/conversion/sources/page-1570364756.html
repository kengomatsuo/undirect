Need to save an address or phone number for later use? Or maybe you use a line of text so frequently it's useful to have it right at your fingertips. With the AutoSnap app you can easily save messages for later and send them again by tapping the globe icon on your keyboard. In some apps, such as snapchat, AutoSnap will even send your message with a single tap! Main features: • Custom clipboard with sentence presets and dozens of fonts • Emojify: insert emojis depending upon text entered if any word is closely related to some emoji icons • Auto paste with adjustable pasting speed If you like the app, feel free to leave a review below! You can unlock unlimited sentence additions with a lifetime one-time purchase. Enjoy! Privacy Policy: https://titano.lol/privacy-policy/ Terms of Use: https://titano.lol/terms-and-conditions/
