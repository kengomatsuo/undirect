Type less. Say more. LazyBoard turns the messages you send every day into one-tap keys, right inside your keyboard, in any app. Save replies once, then send them in a single tap: "On my way!", "Thanks for your order", your address, your email signature, support answers, and more. WHY PEOPLE USE LAZYBOARD • Reply 10x faster: insert full sentences, paragraphs and emails with one tap. • Templates that fill in the details: write "Hi {Name}, thanks for your order" once, then type the name and send. • A place for every phrase: organize replies into color-coded folders. • Paste photos & GIFs: save images and GIFs and send them from the keyboard or iMessage. • Clipboard folder: keep copied text and reuse it anytime. • Fast search: find any saved phrase in seconds. • Synced with iCloud: your phrases stay up to date on iPhone, iPad and Mac. PERFECT FOR Customer support, sales, small businesses, recruiters, real estate, delivery and anyone who answers the same questions every day. PRIVACY-FRIENDLY Your phrases work without Full Access. Photos, GIFs and the clipboard need Full Access, which iOS requires for those features. Your data stays on your device and in your iCloud — never shared. Free to start. Upgrade to Pro for unlimited keys and folders, photos & GIFs, and sync. Questions or ideas? We'd love to hear from you: lazyboardapp@gmail.com Subscription terms: • Payment is charged to your Apple ID account at confirmation of purchase. • Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. • Manage or cancel anytime in your Account Settings.     May 5
    EXPERT RATER

    I use this app primarily for organizing my memes. Soooo buggy though...1) The "icons" that are SUPPOSED to be editable for the folders, are not :(. 2) App glitches 24/7. I click a photo on the keyboard & it doesn't copy. Super annoying. I was prepared to buy the lifetime deal but not sure if it's even worth it if such obvious bugs are allowed to fester. Like these aren't TINY bugs when they completely impact the whole purpose & functionality. It should be free until it's fixed.Aside from that, and to have a lot of potential... But it feels half baked.

    Developer Response

    Hi,Sorry about your experience — I’ll address both points:1. Folder icons are editable, but the Media folder is currently fixed by design. That said, we agree it should be customizable and will update this in a future release.2. If images aren’t copying from the keyboard, it’s usually because “Allow Full Access” isn’t enabled. If it is enabled and the issue still happens, please contact us at lazyboardapp@gmail.com so we can investigate and fix it.We take issues like this seriously and are actively improving the app. Thanks for your feedback.
