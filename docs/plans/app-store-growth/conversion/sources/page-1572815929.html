Very small and efficient app. "Copy and Paste" For Keyboard. Fancy input effects！！！ - Common List for Chat, Fancy input effects！！！ Copy and paste frequently used chat sentences and add them to the "common" list, with fancy input effects 【repeat/emoji/vertical/echo】, especially suitable for social scenes. - Personal List for Registration Edit personal information in advance in the "personal" list of the keyboard, such as name, telephone number, email address, social, etc., especially for scene such as website registration and online ticket purchase. - Passwords List You can save the corresponding passwords for some special websites or software, hide them on the keyboard, and avoid forgetting the password. Simplify your "Copy and Paste" process. Make your chat more fun by fancy input effects. For more technical support, please contact copypaste@outlook.com
