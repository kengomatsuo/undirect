AutoPaste Keyboard is a free, fast and easy keyboard app to help you easily copy paste pre-saved text directly from your keyboard without having to switch to another place to copy and paste the same text. If you find yourself often losing time typing the same text over and over again like emails, addresses, phone numbers, etc. - then this free keyboard app is the perfect solution to save you time. • Automatically paste and send text • Beautiful keyboard • Free, fast and easy pasteboard • Powerful and robust clipboard You can have fun trolling and spamming your friends, or you can save time at work with this robust clipboard to be more productive. Some people use this as a spamming keyboard app to spam their friends for fun. For example: "I miss the rage" is a popular phrase among users on Tiktok. Other people use it to type faster working. Enable the Auto Paste Mode and Auto Send option for faster typing speed.
