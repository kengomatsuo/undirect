## 5. Reply templates

Post only in threads that ask for help, and say you are the developer when you mention Undirect.

### Reply 1, "How do I stop Safari redirects?"
Work through these first, they cost nothing. Settings > Apps > Safari (older iOS: Settings > Safari): turn on Block Pop-ups and Fraudulent Website Warning. Then Clear History and Website Data. Remove any Safari extension you do not recognise. Check Settings > General > VPN & Device Management for a profile you did not install. If one site keeps doing it, leave it; the page itself is the cause. I make a Safari extension, Undirect ($2.99), that targets this one problem, if the steps above are not enough.

### Reply 2, "A fake 'virus detected' page opens when I tap a link"
Close the tab without tapping anything on the page, then clear history and website data in Safari settings. The page is not scanning your phone; no website can. If it began after you installed an app or a profile, remove that. Don't call numbers or install anything it offers. I'm the developer of Undirect, which stops pages from taking taps, but the steps above come first.

### Reply 3, "Pop-ups are blocked but I still get sent to ads"
Block Pop-ups only covers new windows. Many redirects come from a transparent layer over the page or a link that changes under your finger, and the switch does not see those. A content blocker helps with ads on the page. For the tap-hijack case I made Undirect ($2.99, Safari extension, not an ad blocker); I'm the developer, so test it on a site that does this before trusting me.
