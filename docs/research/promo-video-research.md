### Music and sound licences

- G1. https://mixkit.co/license/#musicFree (2026-11-20) Mixkit Stock Music Free License allows social media video posts and online marketing ads, no attribution; forbids remixing into a music-only track and registering on a rights-management service; a YouTube claim is forwarded to team@mixkit.co. Take: Mixkit music is usable in organic posts and paid ads alike.
- G2. https://mixkit.co/license/#sfxFree (2026-11-20) Mixkit Sound Effects Free License covers social posts, ads, broadcast and games; only redistribution of the bare item as stock is barred. Take: Mixkit whooshes, impacts and glitches are the safest SFX source for the off-to-on switch.
- G3. https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/ (2026-11-20) Pixabay licence: free, commercial use, no attribution, modification allowed; bars standalone redistribution and misleading use; page says nothing about Content ID. Take: usable, but keep the per-track licence file for disputes.
- G4. https://pixabay.com/service/faq/ (search result, 2026-11-20) Pixabay's own FAQ/blog notes some music may trigger YouTube Content ID and is cleared by showing the licence. Take: avoid Pixabay music on YouTube Shorts unless the licence PDF is saved; SFX carry less risk (GUESS).
- G5. https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/commercial-music-library (2026-11-20) TikTok Commercial Music Library: pre-cleared, about 1 million tracks, free to businesses, and businesses must use it instead of the general library for organic posts, ads and branded content. Take: on TikTok a business account picks from CML only; the page does not say CML tracks can leave TikTok, so do not export a CML track into a film posted elsewhere.
- G6. https://uppbeat.io/blog/royalty-free-and-copyright-free-music/copyright-safe-music-for-shorts-reels-and-tiktok-how-to-post-without-claims-or-mutes (2026-11-20) Uppbeat: every plan covers YouTube, TikTok, Instagram; Free plan requires a credit line; paid ads need Pro; one download covers one video; Channel Safelist works on YouTube only. Advises tracks with an instant drop, vocal hook or loopable beat. Take: for a 15 s loop, pick a track that starts on the hook, not an intro.

### Undirect 1. RTX OFF / RTX ON

**Origin.** Know Your Meme, https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/rtx-off-rtx-on (read 2026-11-20).
Nvidia showed GeForce RTX at Gamescom 2018 with Battlefield V and Tomb Raider
demos labelled RTX Off / RTX On; Reddit (mostly /r/pcmasterrace) turned it into a
"resolution comparison" meme within days. Top post (38,000 points, 22 Aug 2018)
was the Shitter's Clogged girl. The meme stills keep Nvidia's own badge: a small
dark-grey slab reading "RTX OFF" and a lime-green slab reading "RTX ON", in a
corner, panels stacked or side by side. The joke is the exaggeration: the ON
side is absurdly over-rendered (untooned Patrick Star, a hyper-real Minecraft
cow), not a subtle improvement. Still: `u-kym-rtx-1.png`.
The 2026 spin-off "DLSS 5 Off / DLSS 5 On" (Know Your Meme editorial, published
17 Mar 2026,
https://trending.knowyourmeme.com/editorials/guides/what-is-the-dlss-5-on-vs-off-meme-the-viral-memes-inspired-by-nvidias-controversial-new-ai-graphics-tech-explained)
keeps the same labels but flips the punchline (ON is worse), which shows the
label pair itself is now a recognised template.

**Nvidia's own vertical version.** @nvidiageforce TikTok, "RTX Remix for
Minecraft: Java Edition", posted 2026-10-23, 65 s, 1080x1920, 139K views, 10.2K
likes. https://www.tiktok.com/@nvidiageforce/video/7699987403943038222
- 0.0-1.25 s: one still Minecraft scene, dark-grey "RTX OFF" badge bottom-left. No text otherwise.
- 1.25-2.25 s: a thin lime vertical line sweeps left to right; behind it the ON render replaces OFF (a wipe, not a cut). The badge swaps to lime "RTX ON", moving to bottom-right.
- 2.25-4.65 s: ON holds. Then the same OFF-wipe-ON beat repeats on new scenes; later shots are ON-only with the lime badge always present.
- Last frame: lime bar on the left with the "NVIDIA GEFORCE RTX" lockup on black. No price, no CTA text.
- Stills: `u-nvidia-tiktok-1.png` (OFF), `u-nvidia-tiktok-wipe.png` (mid-wipe), `u-nvidia-tiktok-2.png`, `u-nvidia-tiktok-3.png` (end card).

**Fan example, hard cut on the beat.** @todayisdelta, "Ashley join the trend",
posted 2026-03-08, 18 s, 533K views, 49K likes, sound "MALA" by 6ix9ine.
https://www.tiktok.com/@todayisdelta/video/7614979358368763157
- 0-8.3 s: four or five old Resident Evil 4 (2005) close-ups of Ashley, cut every 1.4-3.3 s, white text "RTX OFF :" top-left, no badge art.
- 8.3 s: hard cut on the song's drop to the 2023 remake's Ashley, text "RTX ON :"; ON shots then cut every 1.3-2 s to the end.
- The OFF side is the dated, low-poly version; the ON side is the glossy one. The whole contrast is carried by the picture and the beat, the label is tiny.
- Last frame: an ON shot, no card. Stills: `u-tiktok-rtxtrend-1.png` (OFF), `u-tiktok-rtxtrend-2.png` (ON).

**Gaming creator, split then slider.** @gamingbetazone, "Ray Tracing ON vs OFF in
Spider-man 2", posted 2025-02-05, 41 s, 170K views.
https://www.tiktok.com/@gamingbetazone/video/7468009537711230240
- 0-4 s: top/bottom split, OFF above, ON below, a lime divider, Nvidia-style slanted badges top-left of each half.
- 5 s onward: full-frame shots where a lime vertical line slides across and the badge flips OFF/ON several times per scene (toggle, compare, toggle back).
- No end card. Stills: `u-tiktok-spiderman-1.png` (split), `u-tiktok-spiderman-2.png` (slider).

**What makes the format work (from the four above).**
1. The badge is the brand: the same two-state label in the same corner every time, grey for OFF, saturated colour for ON.
2. OFF comes first and is held long enough to register (1.25 s in Nvidia's cut, 8 s of build-up in the trend).
3. The switch is either a wipe line (Nvidia, creator) or a hard cut on a musical drop (trend). Both make the moment of switching the event.
4. Exaggeration: the meme's ON side is overdone on purpose; the comic posts make OFF look as bad as possible.
5. No brand ad outside Nvidia using the template was found in two searches; Linus Tech Tips posted "RTX off, RTX on" on 5 Sep 2018 (https://twitter.com/linustech/status/1037513253352423424, seen in search results, not opened). So "brands borrowed it for ads" is unproven.

### Music craft and tempo

- G40. https://blog.audionetwork.com/the-edit/music/music-for-vertical-video (2026-11-20) Audio Network: viewers decide in one to three seconds, so the track must hook as fast as the picture; skip tracks with long intros; pick ones with clean outros that loop back into the opening beat, without abrupt stops or dramatic fades; TikTok and Shorts are sound-on, Reels often muted; cut on kicks, claps, bass hits and vocal chops. ### Cutling: design rules the references support

1. **Open on the ask, not on our heading.** Frame 1 is the moment someone needs the detail: a chat bubble "Could you send me your bank details?" (C2, C3), the viewer's own habit said aloud (C11 0-4 s), or a messy message already on screen (C10). Proposition inside 3 s (C21, C22). Drop "Your bank number" as a title card.
2. **The UI fills the frame.** A full-bleed recording (C2, C3, C12) or a real phone held close (C11, C13, C14). No small device floating on a pastel gradient: that is Rocket Typist's look (C5) and Paste's 115-237-view Shorts (C6). Screen Studio's rule "Nothing stays too small to read" (G17) says the same.
3. **Show the slow way at real speed, then the one tap, on the same shot, labelled.** "(Before)" / "After" captions on one shot reached a 4.0% save rate (C13); letter-by-letter typing first is what makes the fast way land (C14). For the RTX format, use the dark "OFF" / lime "ON" slab and a wipe between the two states (Undirect section, RTX entry); the "OFF" side is Notes-digging plus retyping, the "ON" side is one keyboard tap.
4. **One line of copy per beat, in the top band, swapped on the cut.** Clip Keyboard changes its one-line headline per beat ("Reply with a template, instantly" > "Bank details in one tap" > "Paste the tracking number you copied", C2); Yoink uses one band per feature (C4). Keep it short enough to read, at 5-10 words per second (C21) and inside the 240 px top / 660 px bottom safe zone (G38).
5. **Use a real conversation as the stage.** Both keyboard rivals with previews tell the story as Messages: question bubble, then the answer bubble one tap later (C2, C3). Smoothspeak and iGrammar also stage it in a chat (C9, C10). The answer bubble arriving is the payoff frame.
6. **13-23 s, one use case per film.** Every tip over 250K views ran 15-37 s, and the two biggest ran 15 s and 23 s (C11, C12). Everything over 60 s stayed under 14K (C16). Clip Keyboard fits three use cases into 19 s, but its second half is setup (C2, C3).
7. **Pick a deadline moment.** The ticket-war video (C13) had the highest save rate. Moments where seconds matter (checkout, a ticket rush, someone waiting on a payment) make the before/after a race, not a demo. (Which moment works best for Cutling: GUESS, untested.)
8. **End on proof and the badge; price only as one line, never alone.** No rival or keyboard ad ends on a price (C20). They close on name plus tagline (C2), the App Store listing card with rating (C9) or the badge (C10). Apple forbids a price in the store preview (C18) and wants the badge subordinate and static (C19). For social, "$4.99 once, no subscription" can sit as one line beside the badge, sold as "nothing renews" (C17). That last part is a GUESS until tested.
9. **No tilting or spinning device mockups.** Apple's marketing rules ban animated, angled or 3D-rendered Apple devices and want the app open at the start of the sequence (C19). If we show a device, it is straight-on and still; otherwise the recording goes full-bleed.
10. **Show the face or the hand, not a slideshow.** The top organic tips and keyboard ads are a person or a hand on a real phone (C9, C11, C13, C14), and Admiral reports creator-led openings outperform polished brand openings (C21). Apple bars hands only in the App Store preview (C18), so the store cut stays screen-only and the social cut can use a hand.
