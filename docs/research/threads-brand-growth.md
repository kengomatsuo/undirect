# Growing @monholtco on Threads from zero, and company account against person

Question (owner, 2026-11-25): "Also threads also find the playbook pls until now literally 0 views.
maybe need follows, just as x needs the let's connect posts? but this is company, not individual
account, right? How should we go then?"

Account facts at the start (from `x-reach-drop-2026-11-25.md`): @monholtco on Threads has 0
followers; every post has 0-13 views (short videos, photo carousels, open questions), each with our
own reply under it carrying the site or App Store link, most in the Tech Threads community, several
in languages other than English (id, ja, zh-Hant, pt-BR, ar) on the same account. Instagram
@monholtco: 0 followers; Reels reach 90-135 strangers.

Already in the repo, not repeated here: Meta's Threads feed system card and the Oct 2025 "lively
conversations" change (`social-formats-2026.md`, Threads); one topic per post, communities, links,
the link card only on text posts, the AI label (`short-video-platforms.md`, Threads); viewer
country as a ranking signal (`per-language-accounts.md`); founders out-reaching their company
accounts on X (`x-studio-content-plan.md`, section 2); the X routine (`x-growth-from-zero.md`, 6).

Each finding names its source and the date read. Anything without a source is marked GUESS. Vendor
blogs that sell growth or scheduling tools are marked "vendor, weak". Written as findings land.

### 5. Company account against a person, on Threads and on X

- **Neither platform's ranking reads the account type.** Meta's Threads feed card (updated
  2025-03-07, https://transparency.meta.com/features/explaining-ranking/ig-threads-feed/, re-read
  2026-11-25) names no account type, account age or follower count among its signals; it ranks on
  what the viewer engaged with, including "the viewer's engagement rate with authors". X's code has no
  brand rule either (`x-account-health-and-practice.md`, section 2). So a company handle is not
  penalised; what differs is how people answer it.
- **What Threads' own people say.** Blanc (Threads PMM, 2024-09-27, section 1): "Brands should
  approach Threads with a similar mindset as creators". Hayes (head of Threads, 2026-02-13, section
  1): accounts that underperform "feel performative rather than native to the platform"; his growth
  example is a fan who became a community's organiser. Meta's tips: funny content and conversation
  get the views. Nothing official says a brand must post as a person.
- **Measured gaps between a founder and a company account.** On X, already in the repo:
  founders' posts drew 5-20 times the likes of their company's account on the same news
  (openstatus: brand 7 likes, founder 64; `x-studio-content-plan.md`, section 2). On LinkedIn,
  RevBoss (vendor, weak; sells founder-led marketing): founder accounts 2.1 times the median
  impressions of company pages, 1,122 against 80 posts, Jan-Aug 2026
  (https://www.revboss.com/founder-led-marketing-benchmarks, search snippet, 2026-11-25). No
  Threads-specific comparison was found; one single-market survey (Marketing-Interactive via
  10lab.ai, a Threads marketing seller, Hong Kong, 2025, no sample size) says "68.4% of users prefer
  following personal accounts and don't actively follow brands"
  (https://10lab.ai/en/blog/threads-hong-kong-audience-profile/), too thin to lean on.
- **What one-person app makers on Threads do (section 4):** post as themselves ("I made", "I
  built", their own sales numbers), and the one big hit (@xilantra) came from the maker's own voice
  under someone else's post about the pain, with the brand kept on Instagram.
- **Option against option, for @monholtco:**
  - *The maker's "I" under the company handle* is already the house voice (posts say "I", never
    "we"). Ranking does not care about the handle; people answer a person. What the account lacks
    is the person: a logo avatar, bio "Apps that do exactly what they say.", and posts that read as
    ads (price, app name, "What do you retype most?"). GUESS: a bio that says who is speaking ("I make
    Undirect and Cutling, two small apps for Safari and typing") and posts that tell what happened to
    the maker convert the same handle into a person enough for replies to land.
  - *A personal account beside the brand*: the X evidence says founders out-reach brands, but it
    doubles the reply work that is the only proven input, and it starts at zero too. Whether Meta
    discounts engagement between accounts of one owner is not documented (X removes it,
    `filterLinkedUserEdges`). Threads accounts no longer need an Instagram account since May 2025
    (https://opentermsarchive.org/en/memos/threads-expands-profile-creation-beyond-instagram-users/,
    search snippet). GUESS: worth it only if the owner wants a public name and face; not as a second
    channel run by the same hands.
  - *Both*: the founders in the X sample run both, with the brand mostly quiet and the person doing
    the talking. At zero followers on both, that is the second option plus upkeep.
- **Follow trains on Threads ("let's connect, follow back").** Meta's spam standard bans
  "Attempting to or successfully selling, buying, or exchanging for engagement, such as likes,
  shares, views, follows" and posting or engaging "at very high frequencies"
  (https://transparency.meta.com/policies/community-standards/spam/, read 2026-11-25). DK: follow-for-follow
  "inflates the number and destroys your engagement rate". いんとぷ屋 grew with no follow rounds. On X
  the makers' "let's connect" growth came from talking to builders in those threads, and their first
  followers were builders, not buyers (`x-growth-from-zero.md`, 4.7). On Threads the same honest
  version is answering "share your Mac app" and "what are you building" posts as a maker when the
  host asks, not swapping follows.
- **The Instagram link.** At sign-up, Instagram followers already on Threads get a notification to
  follow (Buffer, https://buffer.com/resources/how-to-use-threads/, search snippet); Hayes calls
  Meta's cross-promotion in Instagram and Facebook feeds Threads' best channel. With 0 Instagram
  followers there is nothing to carry over; it becomes a lever only after Instagram has followers.

## 6. Verdict and routine

### 6.1 The answer

- **Why 0 views:** nothing is wrong with the account (Account Status clean, section 3). Threads
  gives each new post a small audition among strangers and widens it only when people engage
  (Hayes, section 1). Each @monholtco post got its audition (1-13 strangers), drew no like, reply or
  follow, and stopped. With 0 followers there is no second audience. And the account has never
  replied on anyone else's post, which is where Meta says almost half of Threads' views happen.
- **Follows are not the lever; replies are.** Meta's ranking reads what the viewer engaged with,
  not whom the author follows. Following people fills our feed with posts to answer and notifies
  them; follow swaps are banned as "exchanging for engagement" (section 5). The Threads version of
  X's reply work is stronger than on X: Mosseri says reply "much more than you post", Meta says
  replies are about half of views, Hayes says replies drive "disproportionate reach", and the one
  side-by-side count gave a follower per 4.3 comments on Threads against 11.6 on X (Scout7, vendor).
- **Company or person:** no ranking rule treats a company account differently on either platform;
  people answer a person. Keep one account, @monholtco, written as the maker ("I made", "I hit"),
  with a bio that says who is talking and no ad copy in posts. A personal account is the owner's
  call on showing his name and face, not a growth requirement (section 5).

### 6.2 Daily routine for @monholtco on Threads (about 45-60 minutes)

1. **20 replies a day on other people's posts**, in two or three sittings, one at a time, never
   pasted in bursts (Meta's spam standard names engaging "at very high frequencies"). Numbers: DK
   20 a day (vendor); Threadify 10-15 (vendor); Mosseri "much more than you post". Where:
   - Posts from the last few hours, while they are still in their audition (Hayes; posts over three
     days old are no longer recommended to non-followers; "a few hours" is GUESS).
   - Communities: Tech Threads, Build in Public, iPhone and Apple ones; makers' "share your Mac
     app" and "what are you building" posts (@xilantra's, section 4); questions like @jakemarsh's
     "first app you install on a fresh Mac?"; anyone posting about Safari ad tabs, redirects,
     pop-ups or retyping the same text.
   - A working list of about 25 accounts: 10 with 5,000-50,000 followers, 10 peers near our size,
     5 large (DK's split; vendor, weak).
   - Each reply carries a fact, a number or a story detail the poster did not have (DK's rule).
     No link and no app name unless someone asks (GUESS; Meta lists repetitive content as a spam
     indicator, and an unasked plug reads as an ad).
2. **One original a day**, at most two, more at weekends (Meta: at least 2-5 a week, more posts
   go with more views per post, weekends higher). Written as the maker: what happened, a number, a
   mistake, something funny; text with a screenshot or clip, since posts with text plus media
   average more views (Meta). One topic tag. No price and no "What do you retype most?" closer from
   a stranger account (zero answers on all ten such posts, section 3).
3. **At most one quote a day** of a stranger's post about the pain, with the maker's one line
   and the link: "I built a Safari extension for exactly this" (@xilantra: 213K views from one
   such quote, section 4).
4. **Answer every reply within the hour** (Buffer: +42% engagement on posts where the author
   replies; DK and Threadify the same rule).
5. **Follow 5-10 a day**: people we had an exchange with and Apple-app makers. Never a follow
   train or follow-back swap (Meta spam standard).
6. **Weekly**, from Insights: viewers, the followers/non-followers split, net followers,
   interactions, and replies sent. A format with 0 interactions for two weeks is dropped.

**Expected (GUESS from the logs):** 12-21 flat days (DK 12, Threadify "days 8-21"). At 20
replies a day, Scout7's 4.3 replies per follower gives about 4-5 followers a day, so roughly 100 in
a month; DK reached 1,800 in 45 days from 3 posts and 20 replies a day.

### 6.3 Earlier decisions this changes

- `social-formats-2026.md`, Threads: "end a post on something a reader can answer from experience".
  Ten posts did that and got 0 replies; from a zero-follower account the question has nobody to
  answer it. Keep questions for replies on other people's posts and for posts once there are
  followers.
- `social-lessons.md`, Threads: posting films and carousels to Tech Threads with the link as reply
  2/2 is the whole Threads plan today. Originals stay, but at one a day behind 20 replies; the link
  reply is not shown to hurt (Blanc: links do not affect distribution) and stays.
- The X routine's "no link-thread replies" holds on Threads for follow trains, but "share your Mac
  app" posts that ask for apps are on-topic answers, not trains.

## 7. What happened when the routine started (2026-11-25)

On day one @monholtco sent 8 replies, 7 follows and about 8 likes in 33 minutes (10:36-11:09 WIB), replies 1-6 minutes apart. Threads removed the eighth under its spam rule ("We don't allow people to take actions at high volume that might bother others"); Account Status showed no feed or feature limit afterwards. The removed reply was also a feature tip the poster had not asked for. Section 6.2's "20 replies a day" comes from personal accounts with history (DK, vendor) and says nothing about rate; a zero-follower brand account doing it in one burst reads to Meta as automation. Changed pace (Support/social/threads-reply-sitting.md): at most 5 replies a day, 2 a sitting, 30 minutes apart, 3 follows and 5 likes a day, only on posts that ask for something, and Account Status read before each sitting. Raised only after two clean weeks and the owner's go.
