## What it is not

- Not a search ban: `search/recent?query=from:monholtco` returns the latest ten posts.
- Not HQ: `hq_posts` is empty (HQ posted nothing to X); HQ only reads X through the API and sent one answer (Nov 23 to @uzzy_dev1). Reading through the API does not touch reach.
- Not links: the Nov 23-25 posts carry only an attached photo (`t.co` to the post's own media), except the Nov 23 video post, which links undirect.matsuokengo.com.

## What it is

1. The reply work stopped after Nov 21. Replies in other people's fresh threads were the account's only way in front of people who do not follow it; with 4 followers, an original post is shown to almost nobody else.
2. The new posts are open questions with a photo that nobody answered. A post with no likes or replies in its first hour gets no further distribution, and the questions are written for an audience the account does not have yet.
