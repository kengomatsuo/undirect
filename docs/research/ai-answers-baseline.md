## What the answers say about the gaps

- "Best redirect blocker" (U4) goes to AdGuard in all five answers. Those picks cite listicles and forums: canyonwire.com, appshunter.io's Safari redirect extension ranking, adblock-tester.com, lilachbullock.com, itechguides.com, Reddit r/Safari, Blindfold. Undirect is on none of them.
- Claude (U4) and Gemini (U2, U4) say Safari extensions cannot catch window.open or every redirect, so they send people to content blockers. A page that shows a Safari web extension stopping a tap-triggered new tab answers that claim directly.
- Undirect only surfaced where an answer read App Store listings (Perplexity, Google). ChatGPT cited App Store listings for AutoClose, Wipr 2, KeepText and Taphrase, and Gemini for Paste Snippets Keyboard and WordBoard, so the listing's words are what these assistants match on.
- For Paste alternatives (C4), the answers lean on competitors' own comparison pages: clipboardai.app "Paste App Alternatives: 8 Options Compared", clipbook.app "ClipBook vs Paste", historr.com "Paste Alternative Without a Subscription", favtray.com "Paste App Alternatives for Mac in 2027". Cutling has no page like that on the open web yet, and its own compare page is not cited.
- Clipboard roundups the answers cite: switchingtomac.com "7 Best Clipboard Managers for Mac in 2026", igeeksblog.com, Macfolio, textexpander.com blog, onetapapp.co "Best Text Expander Apps for iPhone in 2026", iDownloadBlog, AlternativeTo.
- Answers vary run to run: Gemini's two runs of C2 shared three of seven apps. Re-runs should compare how often each app appears, not one answer.
