# Freemius

> Freemius is a developer-first technology company that handles payments, tax, subscription management, localization, multi-currency purchases, marketing automation and more, allowing developers to focus on their area of genius — building amazing software products. Freemius acts as a Merchant of Record and provides payment tools for software companies of all kinds, including SaaS, Apps, and WordPress Products. With Freemius you can easily integrate Credit Card, PayPal and iDEAL payments with your software, supporting both subscriptions and one-off purchases, along with paid or unpaid trials. We have a rich API (with OpenAPI spec) and Webhooks for faster integration with SaaS, Applications and any other types of software products.

## Payment Processing & Checkout

Freemius provides a set of [checkout and payment tools](https://freemius.com/checkout/) that can be embedded or linked directly from your product or website. The checkout supports multiple payment methods, allows CSS customization, and handles proration for plan changes automatically.

- [Checkout & Payment Links](https://freemius.com/help/documentation/selling-with-freemius/hosted-checkout/): Pre-built checkout pages optimized for conversion
- [Embedded Checkout](https://freemius.com/help/documentation/selling-with-freemius/freemius-checkout-buy-button/): Overlay checkout from your website or app - useful for purchases without leaving current screen
- Multiple Payment Methods Support: Credit cards, PayPal, and iDEAL
- Optimized For Conversion: Mobile friendly, 2-column support, social proofing with reviews and refund policy, one-click upsells, optional billing cycle selector - All features developed for optimizing conversion
- [Customization](https://freemius.com/help/documentation/selling-with-freemius/applying-css-customization/): Customize the look and feel of the Checkout with CSS to match your brand
- [Proration](https://freemius.com/help/documentation/selling-with-freemius/proration/): Freemius supports automatic prorating discounts for upgrades and downgrades
- [Refund Payments](https://freemius.com/help/documentation/selling-with-freemius/refund-payment/): Learn how to refund payments, including full, partial or just tax refund against valid tax exemption

## Tax Compliance

Freemius manages global tax compliance on your behalf, including US Sales Tax and EU/UK VAT. It automatically calculates taxes based on the buyer’s location, supports reverse charge exemptions through valid VAT numbers, and generates compliant tax invoices. You can also issue tax refunds against verified exemption certificates without reducing your net revenue.

- [US Sales Tax](https://freemius.com/us-sales-tax-and-economic-nexus/): Overview of how Freemius handles US Sales Tax and economic nexus rules
- [EU & UK VAT](https://freemius.com/eu-vat-uk-vat-europe/): Details on Freemius's compliance with EU and UK VAT regulations and issuing compliant invoices with VAT numbers and local currencies
- Tax Refunds: Issue compliant refunds for [US Sales Tax](https://freemius.com/blog/changelog/new-feature-us-sales-tax-refund-capability-against-exemption-certificate/) or [EU/UK VAT](https://freemius.com/blog/changelog/improvements-to-the-vat-refund-feature/) when customers provide valid tax exemption documentation

## Marketing & Growth

Freemius includes built-in tools to help retain customers and improve conversions—such as cart recovery, dunning for failed payments, targeted discounts, and automated review collection. It also offers an affiliate platform for managing partner-driven sales.

- [Cart Abandonment Recovery](https://freemius.com/help/documentation/marketing-automation/cart-abandonment-recovery/): Automated system that tracks customers who start checkout but don't complete it, then sends up to 3 recovery emails at strategic intervals (1 hour, 1 day, and 4 days) with optional discount coupons to recover lost sales
- [Dunning & Failed Payments](https://freemius.com/help/documentation/marketing-automation/dunning-failed-payments/): Automated mechanism that handles failed subscription renewals by retrying payments up to 3 times with increasing delays (1 day, 3 days, 5 days), and automatically cancels subscriptions after the 4th failed attempt while providing detailed reporting to developers
- [Special Coupons & Discounts](https://freemius.com/help/documentation/marketing-automation/special-coupons-discounts/): Provide special discounts for exit intent, license recovery, cart abandonment, subscription cancellation, etc. to retain your customers
- [Ratings & Reviews](https://freemius.com/help/documentation/marketing-automation/reviews/): Automatic email requests for reviews and use Freemius for collecting and showcasing real customer reviews
- [Affiliate Platform](https://freemius.com/help/documentation/affiliate-platform/): Freemius includes a fully-featured Affiliate Platform that you can use for free, right out of the box when selling with Freemius, to run your own affiliate program

## [API Documentation](https://docs.freemius.com/api)

Freemius provides a RESTful API that’s fully compliant with the OpenAPI specification. You can use the [OpenAPI schema](https://freemius.com/help/documentation/api/openapi.yaml) to generate client SDKs and integrate with your app. Below are some key endpoints for managing users, licenses, subscriptions, and pricing.

- [List users](https://docs.freemius.com/api/users/list): List and search users by email or Freemius user ID
- [List licenses](https://docs.freemius.com/api/users/list-licenses): List all licenses associated with a user
- [List payments](https://docs.freemius.com/api/users/list-payments): List all payments associated with a user
- [List subscriptions](https://docs.freemius.com/api/users/list-subscriptions): Get all subscriptions associated with a user
- [Activate a license](https://docs.freemius.com/api/licenses/activate): Public endpoint to activate a license for your app
- [Deactivate a license](https://docs.freemius.com/api/licenses/deactivate): Deactivate a license from your app
- [Generate upgrade link](https://docs.freemius.com/api/licenses/generate-upgrade-link): Create authorized Checkout links for license or subscription upgrades
- [Retrieve the pricing table data](https://docs.freemius.com/api/products/retrieve-pricing-table-data): Retrieve structured data needed to render a pricing table in your app

**For the full list of endpoints, refer to the [complete API documentation](https://docs.freemius.com/llms.txt).**
