SOURCE https://midtrans.com/pricing  (page text read in Claude in Chrome, fetched 2026-11-23; JS-rendered table)
Transparent pricing: setting up is free, charged only for successful transactions.
All charges are exclusive of VAT (PPN), except for QRIS, GoPay, and ShopeePay.
BANK TRANSFER (BCA, BNI, Mandiri, Permata, BRI, CIMB VA, Danamon VA, BSI VA, SeaBank, Bank Saqu): IDR 4,000 /transaction
E-WALLETS: GoPay 2%; QRIS 0,7%; ShopeePay 2%; DANA 1.5%; OVO 1,5%  (each /transaction; "Gaming & digital product companies are subject to different rates.")
CREDIT CARD: 2,9% + IDR 2,000 /transaction. No additional fees other than card transaction fees.
OVER THE COUNTER: Indomaret IDR 5,000; Alfamart/Alfamidi/DAN+DAN IDR 5,000 /transaction
CARDLESS CREDIT: Akulaku PayLater 1.7%; Kredivo 2%
Tabs: Online Payments | Withdrawal | All payment methods
Blog text on same page (Indonesian): no subscription fee, no integration fee, Core API free. Fees + VAT are deducted directly in the Midtrans account so withdrawals are net. All transaction data with fee detail is visible in the Midtrans Dashboard.
Same blog text lists (older figures, differ from table): ShopeePay 1.5%. Payouts/disbursement: bank account Rp5.000 per transaction; GoPay Rp1.000 per transaction.
Fee handling: seller may add fee to gross_amount to pass on to buyer.
