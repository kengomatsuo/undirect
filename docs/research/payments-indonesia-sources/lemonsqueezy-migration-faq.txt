FAQs
Lemon Squeezy wind-down FAQs
Find answers to common questions about Lemon Squeezy winding down and migrating to Stripe Managed Payments.
Announcement details
Is Lemon Squeezy shutting down?
Yes. Lemon Squeezy is winding down. Every active Lemon Squeezy store must migrate to Stripe Managed Payments or another provider by March 31, 2027.
Do I need to do anything now?
You don’t need to stop processing on Lemon Squeezy today, but we recommend starting your
migration planning
now. The guided migration typically takes
4–8 weeks
.
We won’t be able to extend the
March 31, 2027
deadline. To allow enough time, we recommend starting your migration no later than
mid-January 2027
.
Can I continue processing on Lemon Squeezy?
Yes. Your existing Lemon Squeezy store will remain operational until March 31, 2027. Lemon Squeezy will send reminders as the deadline approaches.
Can the March 31, 2027 deadline be extended?
Lemon Squeezy will stop processing transactions on March 31, 2027, and we won’t be able to extend that deadline. You can start planning your transition today with
our migration guide
, or export your data if you choose to move to another provider.
Will all earned revenue be paid out?
Yes. Revenue from completed transactions will be paid out in full, provided your account is in good standing. Make sure your bank account and payout details are current in your Lemon Squeezy dashboard.
How do I close my Lemon Squeezy account?
If you choose to close your Lemon Squeezy account, we’ll honor the payout of any remaining earnings, even if your balance is below the usual $50 minimum.
Contact our
support team
when you’re ready to close your account. We’ll help you arrange your final payout and guide you through the account closure process.
How will merchants receive historical Lemon Squeezy tax documents?
Tax documents for earnings processed through Lemon Squeezy will be issued through the payout account you used to receive funds, such as Stripe Express, PayPal, or Wise.
You can access the relevant payout account and its tax documents from your Lemon Squeezy dashboard. We recommend downloading and retaining any tax documents and financial records you need before Lemon Squeezy access ends on March 31, 2027.
Migration
How long will migration take, and what is my deadline?
An end-to-end migration is expected to take four to
eight weeks
. This includes preparing your store, notifying consumers, redirecting traffic to your new provider, and cleaning up any subscriptions that remain on Lemon Squeezy. You must complete your migration off Lemon Squeezy by
March 31, 2027
, so we recommend getting started well before the deadline.
When will migration tooling be available?
Migration tooling will be available on December 2, 2026, in the Migration Hub at
app.lemonsqueezy.com/migration
. We’ll email you when it’s ready.
What can the Managed Payments migration tooling help with?
The migration tooling will:
Create a sandbox with copies of your products and Checkout links so you can test your Stripe integration.
Create your products in Stripe automatically.
Migrate your customers automatically and provide a report mapping their Lemon Squeezy and Stripe IDs.
Trigger subscription migration automatically. You will need to review subscription eligibility and match each Lemon Squeezy product to the corresponding Stripe product.
Automatically notify your customers about the update.
What changes when my products migrate?
Variants become individual products:
Stripe doesn’t support product variants, so each Lemon Squeezy variant becomes its own Stripe product.
Descriptions are converted:
HTML formatting is translated, though some errors may occur. Descriptions longer than 500 characters are shortened.
Image limits apply:
Only the first eight images per product are migrated.
Digital fulfillment changed:
Digital fulfillment isn’t automatically set up for migrated products. You’ll need to connect a separate fulfillment solution or build your own using
Stripe webhooks
.
Changes made to your products in Lemon Squeezy or Stripe won’t automatically update on the other platform.
What if I need to start the migration over?
You can restart the migration from your Lemon Squeezy Migration Hub. Restarting deletes the initial sandbox and creates a new one. Once you’re satisfied with the migrated products in your sandbox, you can copy them from sandbox to live mode.
Will all of my subscriptions migrate automatically?
We will migrate eligible subscriptions with as little disruption as possible, but some subscriptions may require customer authorization, updated payment information, or other action. We will provide details about affected subscriptions in the Lemon Squeezy dashboard before you migrate.
Will my customers need to re-enter their payment details when they are migrated?
In most cases, no. Card subscriptions can migrate without customers re-entering their payment details.
PayPal subscriptions cannot migrate as PayPal subscriptions. Customers must repurchase with a supported payment method.
Cash App Pay subscriptions can migrate to Managed Payments, but not to another provider.
Some customers, including those using India-issued cards, must reauthorize their payment method.
The migration tooling will identify subscriptions that require customer action.
What happens to checkout links, embeds, APIs, and webhooks?
All Lemon Squeezy checkout links, storefront URLs, embedded checkouts, API endpoints, and webhook events must transition to Stripe or another provider. There are no automatic redirects.
Before the migration deadline:
Audit every location where Lemon Squeezy links or integrations appear, including websites, email sequences, social media, affiliate links, and in-app purchase flows.
Update integrations using your new provider’s APIs and webhooks.
Replace Lemon Squeezy checkout links with your new provider’s equivalents.
Test the replacement links and integrations before routing live traffic to them.
What if I want to use another provider?
If you need to migrate to a provider other than Stripe, contact the provider directly to understand its migration requirements. Before choosing a provider, confirm that it:
Provides the tax compliance coverage your business needs and clearly explains which obligations remain your responsibility.
Supports businesses incorporated in your country.
Supports the countries where your customers are located and the payment methods you want to offer.
Offers the features your business needs, such as digital order fulfillment, affiliate management, or email marketing.
If you need to migrate data to the new provider, complete the
intake form
so we can assess your needs and help make your transition from Lemon Squeezy as smooth as possible. Only subscriptions paid by card are eligible for migration to another provider.
Managed Payments
Am I eligible for Managed Payments?
Managed Payments is available to businesses based in
supported countries
that sell eligible digital products, such as SaaS, software, and digital downloads. Before you migrate, check
what’s supported
to make sure Managed Payments has the features and payment methods your business needs.
What happens if I have two stores in different countries or regions?
Each store is reviewed separately, based on where its business entity is located. If one store’s business is in a supported country, it can migrate to Managed Payments, even if your other store can’t.
Does Managed Payments support PayPal?
No, Managed Payments does not support PayPal. Customers with subscriptions paid through PayPal must repurchase their subscription with a supported payment method before those subscriptions can migrate.
Is digital fulfillment, including files, links, and license keys, migrated automatically?
No. Managed Payments does not offer license-key management or file and link hosting. You’ll need to configure digital fulfillment through your own hosting or through a third-party solution. The
Stripe App Marketplace
offers several solutions; review the available options to determine what’s right for your business.
Does Managed Payments offer affiliate management?
No. Managed Payments does not offer affiliate management. You can use a third-party provider, such as Dub.co, to support your affiliates. Email Lemon Squeezy support to ask about an available discount code for 50% off your first year.
Will my fees change?
Yes. Lemon Squeezy charges 5% + 50¢ per transaction, plus some additional fees. Managed Payments standard pricing is 3.5% per transaction plus
Stripe payment processing fees
.
Lemon Squeezy merchants who migrate may get a promotional Managed Payments fee of
1.5% through December 1, 2027,
starting when you install the migration toolkit.
Will tax collection and remittance work the same way?
Yes. Like Lemon Squeezy, Managed Payments acts as the merchant of record. Stripe calculates, collects, and remits applicable indirect taxes on your behalf, including sales tax, VAT, and GST.
Where required, Stripe also registers and files tax returns, remits collected taxes to local authorities, and issues tax invoices for sales of digital products. If you migrate to a provider that does not act as the merchant of record, your business will become responsible for tax compliance in all applicable jurisdictions.
What happens if my country is unsupported?
Managed Payments requires your business to be incorporated in a
supported location
.
If you’re considering establishing an entity in a supported country, consult your accountant and tax counsel to determine whether it’s appropriate for your business.
Stripe Atlas
, which helps businesses incorporate in the US, may be an option, and it can be free when you open a
Stripe Treasury
account within 7 days of incorporating.
If you don’t establish an entity in a supported country, you’ll need to migrate to another provider. Contact your chosen provider directly to understand their migration requirements, and complete the
intake form
in your Lemon Squeezy settings so we can assess your needs and help with the transition.
Only subscriptions paid by card are eligible for migration to another provider.
