SOURCE https://support.stripe.com/questions/requirements-to-open-a-stripe-account-in-another-country (Claude in Chrome page text 2026-11-23)
To open a Stripe account in a country other than your primary business country you need: a legal entity registered in that country, a tax ID, a physical location there to receive mail (not a PO box), a phone number, a government-issued ID (passport or driving licence, any country), a working website, and "A physical bank account in that country denominated in a supported transfer currency for that country. This can't be a virtual bank account." Stripe must also support processing payments there. Exceptions only for Canada (USD) and most European countries (EUR).
