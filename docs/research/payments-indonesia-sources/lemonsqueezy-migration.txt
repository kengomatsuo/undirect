Lemon Squeezy is winding down
Understand your migration options and how to prepare your store to move off Lemon Squeezy.
What’s happening
Lemon Squeezy is winding down and will stop processing transactions on March 31, 2027. All Lemon Squeezy stores need to move off the platform by then, and extensions will not be offered.
We recommend moving to
Managed Payments
, Stripe’s merchant of record solution for digital goods businesses. It handles the complexity of selling internationally—including tax compliance, fraud, disputes, support, and more—so you can stay focused on your products and customers.
If your business isn’t eligible, or you rely on features or payment methods that Managed Payments doesn’t support, you’ll need to
move to another provider
.
Key dates
Your store keeps working as usual until March 31, 2027, so you have time to prepare and migrate.

Migration planning guide: How to prepare today
If you are eligible to migrate to Managed Payments, we recommend you start planning for the migration today. The full process, including notifying customers, updating your links and integrations, and cleaning up remaining subscriptions, can take up to 8 weeks. To finish in time, start your migration no later than mid-January 2027.
Use the time before December 2 to work through this checklist:
Confirm eligibility
Managed Payments is available to businesses based in
supported locations
that sell eligible digital products, such as SaaS, software, e-books, digital downloads, and self-paced courses. The full eligibility requirements are available
here
.
You don’t need to create a Stripe account before tooling launches. The migration flow will help you create or connect one. If you’d like to get started early, you can create a Stripe account
now
.
Review what’s supported
Some Lemon Squeezy features work differently on Managed Payments or aren’t available.
Compare Lemon Squeezy and Managed Payments
and note anything your business relies on, especially:
PayPal:
Not currently supported on Managed Payments.
Storefronts:
Not included. You’ll need a separate website or storefront tool.
Digital fulfillment:
Not included. Managed Payments doesn’t deliver files, license keys, or download links. You’ll need a separate solution, and you can learn about fulfillment options
here
.
Affiliates:
Not included. You’ll need a separate affiliate platform, and you can learn about affiliate options
here
.
Checkout:
Managed Payments works with Stripe Checkout and Payment Links. If you use Lemon Squeezy checkout overlays or a custom checkout, plan to update it.
Take stock of your store
Make a list of what you’ll be moving:
Products and discounts:
Which are active, and which you can archive. Keep any discounts attached to active subscriptions.
Subscriptions:
How many you have, and how your customers pay for them.
Product delivery:
Whether Lemon Squeezy delivers files, license keys, or download links for you.
Links and integrations:
Every place Lemon Squeezy appears, including your website, checkout links, email sequences, affiliate links, in-app purchase flows, APIs, and webhooks. None of these redirect automatically.
Reduce subscriptions that can’t migrate
Subscriptions paid with PayPal can’t migrate to Managed Payments. To avoid disruption later, consider removing PayPal from your Lemon Squeezy checkout now, so new subscribers use a supported payment method. You can do this in your dashboard settings on Lemon Squeezy.
Clean up your store
Archive products and discounts you no longer use. Resolve open disputes and past-due subscriptions where you can.
Back up your data
Export the Lemon Squeezy data
you may need in the future, such as customers, orders, and subscriptions. Make sure the contact email in your Lemon Squeezy account is up to date so you receive migration and tax updates.
Plan your customer communications
Your customers will notice a few changes after you migrate to Managed Payments:
They’ll receive at least 14 days’ notice before their subscriptions move.
Charges will appear on statements as LINK.COM* followed by your statement descriptor.
They’ll manage subscriptions and payment methods through Link, Stripe’s consumer platform, instead of the Lemon Squeezy Customer Portal.
They’ll access downloads and license keys through the fulfillment solution you connect.
Update your help pages and plan any messages you want to send your customers before the official notice goes out.
Review your roadmap
Consider whether planned launches or other Q1 work need to move to make room for your migration.
Moving to Stripe Managed Payments
On December 2, 2026, we’ll launch guided migration tooling in the Migration Hub at app.lemonsqueezy.com/migration. We’ll email you when it’s ready.
What the migration tooling does
The tooling is designed to move eligible stores to Managed Payments with as little customer disruption as possible. It will:
Create a Stripe sandbox with copies of your products and payment links, so you can test before changing your live store
Create your products in Stripe and migrate your customers, with a report mapping Lemon Squeezy customer IDs to Stripe customer IDs
Help you review which subscriptions are eligible and match each Lemon Squeezy product to its Stripe product
Notify your customers, giving them at least 14 days’ notice before eligible subscriptions move
Migrate eligible subscriptions automatically
Subscription migration
Before you confirm your migration, the Migration Hub shows which subscriptions can move automatically and flags any customers who need to take action.
Most card subscriptions migrate without customers re-entering their payment details. Some subscriptions need extra steps:
PayPal: PayPal subscriptions can’t migrate. Customers will need to start a new subscription using a supported payment method before their subscription can migrate.
India-issued cards: Customers need to reauthorize their payment method once their subscription moves to Stripe.
Cash App Pay: These subscriptions can migrate to Managed Payments, but not to another provider.
Promotional pricing
Lemon Squeezy merchants who migrate get a promotional Managed Payments price of 1.5% through December 1, 2027. Promotional pricing starts when you install the migration toolkit, as early as December 2, 2026. Standard Stripe payment processing fees also apply.
See Managed Payments pricing
.
Moving to another provider
If Managed Payments isn’t available where your business is based, or it doesn’t support features you need, you’ll need to move to another provider before March 31, 2027.
Before you choose a provider, confirm that it:
Supports businesses based in your country
Supports the countries where your customers are and the payment methods you want to offer
Provides the tax compliance coverage you need, and is clear about which obligations remain yours
Offers the features you rely on, such as digital fulfillment, affiliates, or email marketing
If you move to a provider that doesn’t act as the merchant of record, your business becomes responsible for tax compliance in every applicable jurisdiction.
Some businesses may choose to form a legal entity in a country Managed Payments supports instead.
Stripe Atlas
, which helps businesses incorporate in the US, may be an option, and it can be free when you open a
Stripe Treasury
account within 7 days of incorporating. If you go this route, start right away so you can complete your entity setup and migration to Managed Payments before March 31, 2027. Talk with your tax and legal advisors first.
Get help with your move
Contact your new provider to understand its migration requirements. If you need to move data or subscriptions to another provider,
complete our intake form
(under my account > settings > migration) so we can understand your needs and help make the transition as smooth as possible. Only subscriptions paid by card can be migrated to another provider.
Data exports and tax documents
Export any Lemon Squeezy data you may need before March 31, 2027.
Learn how to export your order data
.
Tax documents for earnings processed through Lemon Squeezy are issued through the payout account you used to receive funds, such as Stripe Express, PayPal, or Wise. We’ll share more information on accessing tax documents after migration, including any actions needed in 2027 and 2028.
