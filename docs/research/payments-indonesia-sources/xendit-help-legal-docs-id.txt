Article: documents differ by business type; additional licenses may be asked depending on industry.
A. General Corporate Business (PT, CV, PMA): Owner/Director's ID Card; Owner/Director's Tax ID number; Corporate Tax ID Card; Business Identification Number (NIB) but can be replaced by TDP and SIUP; Deed of Latest Change / Akta Pengangkatan Direktur Terakhir; SK Menkeh (Ministry of Justice and Human Rights decree) of that deed; Other Business License according to Business Activity. Newly established company with only Akta Pendirian + SK Menkeh Pendirian may submit those.
B. Foundation (Yayasan): same plus Foundation License (TDY).
C. Cooperation (Koperasi): same plus PSE Certificate.
D. Foreign entity operating in Indonesia: "Only Business Entity is allowed to be onboarded as Foreign Business in Indonesia".
The headings listed are General Corporate (PT, CV, PMA), Foundation, Cooperation, Foreign Entity. NO heading for individual / sole proprietor / perorangan appears in this article.
NIB: status must be 'Terbit'; first page with business details; issued date no longer than 5 years; KBLI must match the nature of business; QR codes accessible.
Company Tax ID Card: full name must match business name; NPWP number should be 16 digit.
Deed documents must be PDF with all pages; names must match.
Cannot be verified: blurry, obscured, incomplete, expired, documents not belonging to the business.
