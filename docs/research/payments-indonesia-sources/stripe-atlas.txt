What business structures does Stripe Atlas support? Atlas supports Delaware C corporations and LLCs, including C corporations formed as subsidiaries of an existing company. C corporations are commonly used by startups that plan to raise venture capital or issue stock to employees, while LLCs can be a better fit for businesses that want a more flexible ownership and tax structure. How is Atlas different from other incorporation services? Atlas differs from many other services by offering a comprehensive company legal setup, including bylaws, founder equity issuance, 83(b) election filings, and IP assignment. Atlas incorporation documents were designed with Cooley , a leading Silicon Valley law firm, to meet investor expectations. Atlas companies can then bank, fundraise, and accept payments directly from their Stripe Dashboard before receiving their company tax ID. They also receive more than $200K in partner perks , $2.5K in Stripe credits, and a $500 deposit when opening a Stripe Treasury bank account within 7 days of incorporating. How much does Atlas cost, and how long does it take? Atlas costs $500, including Delaware filing fees and your first year of registered agent service. The application takes less than 10 minutes, and most companies are incorporated within one business day. Your company tax ID typically arrives in one to two business days if you provide a US Social Security number, US company address, and US phone number. Without all three, it can take several weeks. You can start banking, accepting payments, and fundraising on Stripe before your EIN arrives. How does Atlas help my company stay compliant after incorporating? Atlas helps your company stay compliant after incorporating by providing reminders, registered agent service, legal templates, and tools for founder equity. After the first year, registered agent service costs $100 per year. Delaware C corporations must separately pay an annual report fee and franchise tax, while Delaware LLCs pay an annual tax. You’re also responsible for applicable federal and state tax filings, and Atlas does not provide legal or tax advice. Does Atlas come with a Treasury bank account? Not automatically. After incorporating, you can open a Stripe Treasury bank account with no minimums or monthly fees, even before receiving your tax ID (EIN). Do I need to be based in the US to use Atlas? No. Founders from anywhere in the world can use Atlas to form a US company in Delaware, and you don’t need to be a US citizen or resident. You also don’t need a US address or Social Security number to incorporate, although US contact and tax information can speed up tax ID processing and may be required for some banking and Stripe services.
