# Short-video platforms: what each composer offers, disclosure, cadence, links, APIs

Research for @monholtco's Cutling and Undirect promo films, started 2026-11-20.
Each line: fact, then source URL and date read. "Official" = the platform's own
help center, creator academy, newsroom or developer docs. "Marketing source" =
Buffer, Later, Hootsuite, Sprout Social and similar, which report tests and
opinions, not platform rules. "GUESS" = not from a source.

## TikTok (TikTok Studio web upload)

### Web composer options, links

- Checked 2026-11-20: TikTok Studio's upload page (https://www.tiktok.com/tiktokstudio/upload) shows only the file picker until a video is chosen; the post-settings panel was not opened here, to avoid touching the account. Its left menu lists "Inspiration" and "Royalty-free sounds".
- Official API equivalent of the composer fields (which settings exist at all): caption, privacy, allow comment/duet/stitch, cover frame (`video_cover_timestamp_ms`), "Your brand"/"Branded content" disclosure, AI-generated label. Source: https://developers.tiktok.com/doc/content-posting-api-reference-direct-post, read 2026-11-20.
- Marketing sources, not official: the desktop upload page offers caption, hashtags, cover, location tag, privacy, comment/duet/stitch, "Show more" advanced settings (content disclosure lives there), and scheduling for Creator/Business accounts from 15 minutes to 10 days ahead; scheduled posts cannot be edited, only deleted and re-uploaded. Sources: https://www.hopperhq.com/blog/how-to-schedule-tiktok-posts-desktop-mobile/, https://metricool.com/schedule-tiktok-videos/, https://blog.hootsuite.com/what-is-tiktok/amp/ (search snippets, read 2026-11-20).
- Links, marketing sources, not official: URLs in a TikTok video caption are not clickable, so the clickable link lives in the profile "Website" field (personal accounts at 1,000+ followers; Business accounts without the follower minimum, sometimes after business registration). Sources: https://buffer.com/resources/how-to-put-a-link-in-tiktok-bio, https://www.shopify.com/blog/how-to-add-link-to-tiktok-bio (search snippets, read 2026-11-20). No official TikTok source found today says a URL in a caption lowers reach (GUESS: a dead, unclickable URL just wastes caption space; write "link in bio" or the app's name for App Store search instead).

### Cadence, several videos a day, languages

- Official, quoted: "While there's no one-size-fits-all answer, success cases show that publishers who post multiple times a day experience higher video views and exposure on the For You feed. We suggest setting a daily post target you can consistently hit and planning your content around that goal." Source: https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/posting-cadence-best-practices-publishers (last updated Jul 6, 2026), read 2026-11-20 in Chrome. TikTok does not say that several posts in one day hurt reach.
- Official: "The trending rate on TikTok measures how quickly a video is gaining popularity through views, likes, and shares. A high trending rate increases the likelihood of your video being featured on the For You Feed". Same source, read 2026-11-20.
- Official, on duplicates (relevant to one film in several languages): "Content is also ineligible for the FYF if it includes unoriginal or reused material without anything new." Source: https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/integrity-authenticity, read 2026-11-20. Also: "We strictly prohibit automation tools, scripts, or other tricks designed to bypass our systems." and deceptive behaviour can lead to restrictions "in the FYF". Same source.
- GUESS: a dubbed version in another language with its own voiceover, on-screen text and caption is arguably "something new", but nothing official says how the duplicate check treats a translated cut of a video from the same account. Safer: put the other languages on separate days rather than in one burst, and do not post byte-identical files twice.
- Official, quoted: "Even if a video doesn't make it to the FYF, people may still find it through search or by going to a creator's account." and "Creators can check TikTok's analytics to see how their videos are performing, including if there were any made ineligible for recommendation." Source: https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/fyf-standards, read 2026-11-20. Check this after posting.
- Marketing source (Buffer, a TikTok video summarising its own analysis of 11.4 million posts from 150K+ accounts): "Posting 2–5 times per week gives you the most efficient lift (up to 17% more views per post)" and more posts mean more chances of a hit. Source: https://www.tiktok.com/@bufferapp/video/7558851928654974221, search snippet read 2026-11-20.
- Length, marketing source (Buffer, March 2025, 1.1M TikToks): "Videos longer than 60 seconds tend to get at least 43.2% more reach and 63.8% more watch time than shorter videos"; 30-60 s beat 10-30 s and 5-10 s on reach. Buffer's later reports (July 2025, Sept 2026) find 3-10 minute videos highest in median views, while short clips lead on completion rate. Sources: https://buffer.com/resources/longer-tiktoks-get-more-views-data/, https://buffer.com/resources/tiktok-analytics/ (search snippets, read 2026-11-20). Correlational, Buffer-user sample. GUESS for our 20-60 s promos: do not pad them; the official ranking signal is how fast views, likes and shares accrue (trending rate, above), and a short film watched to the end serves that.

### Caption, search keywords, hashtags

- Official: TikTok Search reads the words in and around the video: "use things like your video's description, voiceovers, captions, and on-screen text to make it clear just what your video's all about, so it connects with searchers." Source: https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/article/Getting-discovered-with-Search ("Last updated: Aug 19, 2026"), read 2026-11-20 in Chrome. For our films: say the search phrase in the voiceover, show it on screen, and put it in the description (e.g. "clipboard keyboard for iPhone", "stop Safari redirects", "block pop-up tabs in Safari").
- Official, quoted: "Using a long list of hashtags will help your video rank higher in Search. Not quite! While hashtags can support discovery, more doesn't always mean better. Overloading your caption with loosely related keywords or hashtags can actually work against you." Same source, read 2026-11-20.
- Official, quoted: "adding unrelated text to your video in this way can get your video flagged for spam" (hidden or off-screen keyword text). Same source, read 2026-11-20.
- Official: Creator Search Insights in TikTok Studio suggests hashtags and keywords per search topic ("browse through related terms, and even find suggested hashtags and keywords"). The 'Searches by followers' filter needs 1,000 followers. Same source, read 2026-11-20.
- Official: an API caption can be up to 2,200 UTF-16 characters (https://developers.tiktok.com/doc/content-posting-api-reference-direct-post, read 2026-11-20). The in-app/web limit of 4,000 characters is cited by creators on tiktok.com discover pages, not by a help article found today: unverified.

### Disclosure: AI-generated content

- Official, quoted: "AI-generated content (AIGC) includes images, video and/or audio that is generated or modified by artificial intelligence". Source: https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/creating-videos/ai-generated-content (redirects to https://www.tiktok.com/support/faq_detail?id=7636670084747893268), read 2026-11-20 in Chrome.
- Official, quoted: "we encourage creators to label content that has been either completely generated or significantly edited by AI" and "We also require creators to label all AI-generated content that contains realistic images, audio and video, as explained in our Community Guidelines." Same source, read 2026-11-20.
- Official, quoted: "Note: Turning on the AI-generated content setting won't affect the distribution of your video as long as it doesn't violate our Community Guidelines." Same source, read 2026-11-20.
- Official: an unlabelled realistic AIGC post can be removed: "We may remove content that violates our Community Guidelines policies by depicting misleading information or unlabelled AI-generated content." Same source, read 2026-11-20. And: "misleadingly labelling unaltered content with this label is a violation of our Terms of Service".
- Official: TikTok auto-labels uploads carrying C2PA Content Credentials, and "Once your content is labelled as AI-generated with an auto label, you won't be able to remove the label from your post." Same source, read 2026-11-20.
- Reading for our films (GUESS, interpretation of the quotes above): an AI-generated voiceover is AI-generated "realistic ... audio", so the "require" sentence covers it even though the screen recording is real. The label costs no distribution per TikTok's own note, while leaving it off risks removal. Recommendation: turn on "AI-generated content" for every film with an AI voice.
- Official, Community Guidelines wording (narrower than the help page): "we require creators to label AI-generated or significantly edited content that shows realistic-looking scenes or people. Unlabeled content may be removed, restricted, or labeled by our team, depending on the harm it could cause." Source: https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/integrity-authenticity (Released August 25, 2026, effective September 24, 2026), read 2026-11-20 in Chrome. Read together (GUESS): the guidelines' "scenes or people" wording does not clearly cover a synthetic narrator over a real screen recording, but the help page says "realistic images, audio and video". The two texts disagree, labelling costs nothing in distribution, and leaving the label off carries the removal risk, so label it.

### Disclosure: commercial content ("Your brand" / "Branded content")

- Official: the web upload path is "Upload" > select video > "Show more" > turn on "Disclose commercial content" > choose "Your brand" or "Branded content" > "Post". Once saved, it adds a "Commercial content" label to the post or under the description, and shows "Paid partnership" for branded content promoting a third party. It can also be switched on after posting (profile > post > More options > Ad settings > Disclose commercial content). If the proper disclosure is missing, "your post may be removed or restricted." Source: https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/how-to-turn-on-the-commercial-content-disclosure-setting-in-tiktok, read 2026-11-20 (WebFetch summary; ads.tiktok.com refused page reading in Chrome).
- Official (developer guideline describing the same choice): "Your Brand : You are promoting yourself or your own business. This content will be classified as Brand Organic." The prompt reads "Your photo/video will be labeled as 'Promotional content'". "Branded Content : You are promoting another brand or a third party." The prompt reads "labeled as 'Paid partnership'". Branded content cannot be private. Source: https://developers.tiktok.com/doc/content-sharing-guidelines, read 2026-11-20.
- Official, on reach: the disclosure "will not affect the way TikTok recommends content to users", while undisclosed commercial content flagged by TikTok means "the video may not be eligible for distribution in the For You feed" (the poster gets 24 hours to turn the disclosure on or appeal). Source: https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/about-the-content-disclosure-setting-for-creators, read 2026-11-20 (WebFetch).
- For @monholtco promoting its own apps: pick "Your brand" only, never "Branded content" (that one means a paid deal with a third party and shows "Paid partnership"). Basis: the two official definitions above.
- Marketing/third-party sources, not official: songs in TikTok's general music library are not cleared for brand posts; brands should use the Commercial Music Library, and the Music Usage Confirmation applies when disclosure is on (https://www.foximusic.com/blog/commercial-music-licensing-tiktok-guide/, https://www.lilachbullock.com/tiktok-commercial-music-library/, search snippets read 2026-11-20). Several guides say a disclosure label cannot be edited after posting (search snippet, read 2026-11-20); the official article above does describe turning it on after posting.

## Instagram Reels (Meta Business Suite)

### Ranking, originality, cadence, length, languages

- Official (Adam Mosseri, "Instagram Ranking Explained", May 31, 2023): Reels ranking predicts "how likely you are to reshare a reel, watch a reel all the way through, like it"; Instagram makes less visible "low-resolution or watermarked reels" and "reels that have already been posted on Instagram"; "The same Recommendation Guidelines that apply to Explore apply to reels." Source: https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained, read 2026-11-20 (WebFetch). For us: upload the clean master, never a file that carries a TikTok watermark, and do not re-post the same file twice on Instagram.
- Official (trial reels help page, quoted above): "Your trial reel may get limited reach if Instagram detects that you've previously shared the same content." Source: https://help.instagram.com/1013292530224018, read 2026-11-20.
- Same film in several languages on one Instagram account: no official Instagram statement found today. GUESS from the two lines above: a dubbed cut with the same footage risks being treated as "already posted" content and shown less; space them out, change the opening seconds and the on-screen text per language, and lead with the strongest language.
- Cadence, official: no recent Instagram figure found. Mosseri, June 2021, as quoted by many blogs: "a couple feed posts a week, and a couple stories a day" (e.g. https://www.socialpilot.co/instagram-marketing/how-often-to-post-on-instagram, search snippet read 2026-11-20). Not a 2026 rule.
- Cadence, marketing source (Buffer, Kirsti Lang, Aug 13, 2025, ~2 million posts): "posting 3-5 times per week on Instagram is likely the sweet spot"; "The biggest jump in reach per post happens when you move from 1–2 to 3–5 posts per week"; on extra posts competing with each other: "Neither of these is likely." and "It's unlikely the algorithm will push all your posts out one after the other." Its buckets stop at "10+ posts" a week; posting several in one day is not studied. Source: https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-to-post-on-instagram/, read 2026-11-20.
- Length, marketing source (Buffer, Nov 12, 2026, 2.7 million reels, seen only as a teaser): "Reels between 90 seconds and three minutes reached 4–7% more people than each account's own average." Source: teaser on https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-to-post-on-instagram/, read 2026-11-20.
- Official spec for API-published reels: 3 s to 15 min, 9:16 recommended (https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/instagram-graph-api/reference/ig-user/media, read 2026-11-20).

### Links

- Reported (Engadget via several outlets, March 2026): Meta confirmed it is testing clickable links in post captions for Meta Verified subscribers only; one creator reports a cap of 10 links a month. Sources: https://dataconomy.com/2026/03/13/instagram-tests-clickable-caption-links-for-meta-verified-users/, https://ppc.land/instagram-tests-clickable-links-in-post-captions-for-meta-verified-users/ (search snippets, read 2026-11-20). For a normal account a URL in a Reel caption is plain text, so "link in bio" is the route.
- Whether a URL in a Reel caption lowers reach: no official Meta source found today (GUESS: since the URL is not clickable it adds nothing; leave it out and point to the bio or "search Cutling on the App Store").
### Several languages: Meta's own translation feature

- Reported (TechCrunch, Aug 19, 2025): Meta rolled out "Translate your voice with Meta AI" for Reels, English to and from Spanish, for "Facebook creators with 1,000 or more followers and all public Instagram accounts globally, where Meta AI is offered"; it dubs in the creator's voice with optional lip sync, viewers see the version in their language and can opt out, and insights break views down by language. Hindi and Portuguese were added in October 2025. Sources: https://techcrunch.com/2025/08/19/meta-rolls-out-ai-powered-translations-to-creators-globally-starting-with-english-and-spanish/, https://www.iphoneincanada.ca/2025/10/09/meta-ai-translation-dubbing-for-reels/ (search snippets, read 2026-11-20). Not read on an official Meta page today.
- GUESS, for our case: this is Meta's built-in answer to "one film, several languages": one reel and one set of engagement, rather than separate per-language uploads that may be judged as already-posted content. It is chosen at publish time in the app; whether Business Suite's desktop reels composer offers it was not checked. It only covers EN/ES/HI/PT, so other languages still need their own cut.

### AI label (applies to Instagram, Facebook and Threads alike)

- Official, quoted: "On Facebook, Instagram and Threads, Meta may require an AI label when content has photorealistic video or realistic-sounding audio that was digitally created, modified or altered, including with AI." Source: https://www.meta.com/en-gb/help/artificial-intelligence/1783222608822690/ ("How to identify AI content on Meta products", updated a day before reading), read 2026-11-20 in Chrome.
- Official, decisive for our films: among "Examples of digitally created content that requires a label on Facebook, Instagram and Threads" Meta lists "A reel narrated with a realistic AI-generated voiceover". Same source, read 2026-11-20. So every film with an AI voice must carry the AI label on all three Meta surfaces, even though the screen recording is real.
- Official, quoted: "Note: There may be penalties for content shared without a label when it is required." Same source, read 2026-11-20.
- Official: label wording is now "AI content"; content with C2PA/IPTC signals of AI creation is labelled automatically; signals of AI modification put the note under the post's Options menu instead of on the post. Same source, read 2026-11-20.
- Reach effect of a self-applied post label: no official Meta statement found today either way. Reported: Instagram said its account-level "AI creator" label left "ranking, distribution and recommendations" unchanged during the test, and reach limits were announced for profiles showing AI-generated people *without* the label (https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/417580/instagram-limits-creators-reach-for-profiles-with.html, https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-adds-ai-creator-labels/819267/, search snippets read 2026-11-20). Reports of reach drops from the post label cite no data. GUESS: label and move on; the documented risk is not labelling.

### Trial reels

- Official, quoted eligibility: "you'll need a public Instagram account" and "If you have a professional (business or creator) account, you must have at least 200 followers." "If your public account is ineligible to appear in recommendations, you will also lose access to trial reels." Source: https://help.instagram.com/1013292530224018, read 2026-11-20 in Chrome. If @monholtco has under 200 followers, the Trial toggle will not be offered.
- Official, quoted: "Make sure that you create a new reel. Your trial reel may get limited reach if Instagram detects that you've previously shared the same content." Same source, read 2026-11-20. So a film already posted as a normal reel should not be re-posted as a trial, and the reverse.
- Official, quoted: "After you've shared your trial reel, your followers won't see it in Feed or Reels. Trial reels don't appear in your profile unless you decide to share them to everyone." and "Trial reels are evaluated independently in Instagram's ranking and don't affect the performance or ranking of your standard Reels content." Insights arrive "within 24 hours". Same source, read 2026-11-20.
- Official: "Share to everyone automatically" (under Trial settings) shares the trial "if it's performing well soon after you publish it, based on signals such as views and engagement"; the creators blog puts it as "based on the views it receives within the first 72 hours." Sources: same help page, and https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-trial-reels (December 10, 2024), read 2026-11-20.
- Use for today (GUESS): with five films in a day, sending one or two as trials tests them on non-followers without filling followers' feeds, if the account clears 200 followers. The help page describes the app flow only; whether Business Suite's reels composer shows the Trial toggle is not stated (the API exposes it as `trial_params`).

### Hashtags

- Official wording as quoted by Social Media Today (Dec 18, 2025): Instagram "will gradually update the number of hashtags that you can include in a caption for a reel or post to five" and "using fewer (up to 5) more targeted hashtags, rather than many generic ones" helps. Source: https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagrams-implementing-new-limits-on-hashtag-use/808309/, read 2026-11-20. The API reference still documents "Maximum 2200 characters, 30 hashtags, and 20 @ tags" (https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/instagram-graph-api/reference/ig-user/media, read 2026-11-20), so the five-tag cap is enforced in the apps, not yet in the API docs. Use at most 5.
- Reported, not quoted in full: Mosseri said in 2025 that hashtags "don't improve visibility on Instagram" but are "a great way to let people know what a post is about". Same Social Media Today source, read 2026-11-20.

## Threads (threads.com web composer)

### Topic tag and communities

- Official, quoted: "Tagging a topic in your post allows others on Threads to discover your post and other posts associated with that topic in real time. You can include up to one topic per post." Web steps: "Click Add in the bottom right to start a new thread. Click Tag or type # and then enter a topic", either free text or "choosing from trending topics that appear". A topic "Can be up to 50 characters long" and "May contain spaces and other special characters." Clicking it shows "search results for posts related to the topic." Source: https://help.instagram.com/1356090605000312/, read 2026-11-20 in Chrome. So: one topic per post, chosen to match what people search (e.g. "iPhone tips", "Safari", "Mac apps"), not a hashtag list.
- Official, communities: "Communities grow from popular topic tags." "You can post directly to a community by adding its tag or by opening the composer from the community page." "Even if you don't add a tag, relevant posts may be automatically surfaced into a related community". "If your profile is public, anyone can see your community posts including non-members (for example, in For you)." Beta "across more than 200 of the most popular topics on Threads." Source: https://help.instagram.com/695785840189573/, read 2026-11-20 in Chrome. Action: when typing the topic, prefer a tag that shows the blue community icon (an active community) if one fits, e.g. an iPhone, Apple or Mac community. Whether those exist today was not checked (GUESS).
- Help-centre search also lists "Label AI content on Threads", "About gems on Threads" and "Personalise your For you feed on Threads" (search at https://help.instagram.com/search?query=threads%20topic, read 2026-11-20).

### Links

- Official (API docs): URLs in post text are counted as links; posts may carry at most 5 unique links since December 22, 2025; a link-preview card (`link_attachment`) is only for text-only posts, not video posts. Source: https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/threads/posts.md, read 2026-11-20. So a video post shows the URL in text but no preview card; GUESS: putting the link in a self-reply (a text post) gets the card.
- Reported (Social Media Today, 2025): Mosseri said Threads does not intentionally downrank links but "we don't place much value on" them, and in June 2025: "We've been working on making sure links are ranked properly. Links have been working much better for more than a month now." Sources: https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/meta-says-link-posts-ranked-properly-threads-reach/750126/, https://www.engadget.com/social-media/threads-is-finally-embracing-links-150012499.html (search snippets, read 2026-11-20).

### Video and AI label

- Official: video up to "300 seconds (5 minutes)", 9:16 recommended, text up to 500 characters (API docs: https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/threads/posts.md, read 2026-11-20).
- Official: the Threads composer has an AI-label option ("The published post displays the AI info label, as it does when you select the AI-label option in the Threads composer."), and Meta's rule naming "A reel narrated with a realistic AI-generated voiceover" applies to Threads as well. Sources: https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/threads/posts.md and https://www.meta.com/en-gb/help/artificial-intelligence/1783222608822690/, read 2026-11-20. Turn it on for every film with an AI voice.

## APIs

### TikTok Content Posting API

- Official, quoted: "As a developer, you can use this API in an unverified status, but all content uploaded via this endpoint will be restricted to private viewing mode. To lift this restriction, your API client must undergo an audit to verify compliance with our Terms of Service." Source: https://developers.tiktok.com/doc/content-sharing-guidelines (page "Last updated August 4, 2026"), read 2026-11-20.
- Official, quoted: "User cap : Unaudited API Clients can allow up to 5 users to post in a 24 hour window. All user accounts using the API client to post must be set to private at the time of posting." Same source, read 2026-11-20.
- Official, quoted: "Private Viewership : Unaudited API Clients can only post contents in SELF_ONLY viewership. To make the contents publicly viewable later on, the account owner must first change their account visibility to public, and then change the privacy settings of each content to \"Everyone.\"" Same source, read 2026-11-20. So an unaudited app needs the whole @monholtco account switched to private at posting time: not usable for a public brand account.
- Official, quoted: "Posting cap : There is a limit on the number of posts that can be made to a creator account in a 24-hour window via Direct Post API. The upper limit may vary among creators (typically around 15 posts per day/ creator account) and is shared across all API Clients using Direct Post." Same source, read 2026-11-20.
- Official, quoted, decisive for the owner's case: "API Clients must not be limited to test applications and should be intended for a wide audience, not limited to internal groups/private use. Not acceptable: A utility tool to help upload contents to the account(s) you or your team manages. ❌" Same source, read 2026-11-20. An in-house uploader for @monholtco is exactly what the audit refuses, so the audit route is closed for this use, whatever its duration.

### Instagram Graph API (Reels publishing)

- Official, quoted: "If your app only serves your Instagram professional account or an account you manage, Standard Access is all your app needs." and "Standard Access is the default access level for all apps and limits the data your app can get." Advanced Access ("Your app must complete Meta App Review to be granted Advanced Access") is only needed for accounts with no role on the app. Source: https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/overview/, read 2026-11-20 (WebFetch; quotes extracted verbatim).
- Official, quoted: "All Business, Consumer, and Gaming apps are automatically approved for Standard Access for all permissions and features." and "Permissions with Standard Access can only be requested from app users who have a role on the requesting app." Source: https://developers.facebook.com/docs/graph-api/overview/access-levels/, read 2026-11-20. So yes: a Meta app with the owner as admin can publish Reels to @monholtco without App Review.
- Official: permissions are `instagram_business_basic` + `instagram_business_content_publish` (Instagram Login) or `instagram_basic` + `instagram_content_publish` (+ `pages_read_engagement` with Facebook Login). Source: https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/content-publishing/, read 2026-11-20.
- Official, quoted: "Instagram accounts are limited to 100 API-published posts within a 24-hour moving period." Same source, read 2026-11-20.
- Official: the plain flow needs the video at a public URL ("We cURL media used in publishing attempts, so the media must be hosted on a publicly accessible server at the time of the attempt."), but Reels also take a resumable upload of a local file to `rupload.facebook.com/ig-api-upload/<IG_MEDIA_CONTAINER_ID>`, with one catch, quoted: "This guide shows you how to upload large video files, from local and publicly hosted content, to be published on Instagram. This is available only for apps that have implemented Facebook Login for Business." Sources: https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/instagram-platform/content-publishing.md and https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/instagram-platform/content-publishing/resumable-uploads.md, read 2026-11-20. So: Instagram Login route = public URL needed; Facebook Login for Business route = local upload works.
- Official: flow is POST `/<IG_ID>/media` with `media_type=REELS` (container), poll status until `FINISHED`, then POST `/<IG_ID>/media_publish` with `creation_id`. Same source, read 2026-11-20.
- Official: Reels container parameters include `caption` ("Maximum 2200 characters, 30 hashtags, and 20 @ tags."), `collaborators` (up to 3 usernames), `cover_url` (JPEG under 8 MB, ideally 9:16; overrides `thumb_offset`), `thumb_offset` (ms), `share_to_feed`, `audio_name` (rename the original audio once), `location_id` (a Page with location data), `user_tags`, `trial_params` (`graduation_strategy` = `MANUAL` or `SS_PERFORMANCE`), `is_paid_partnership`, `branded_content_sponsor_ids`, `is_ai_generated`. `alt_text` is not supported for Reels. Reel video: 3 s to 15 min, max 300 MB, 23-60 fps, 9:16 recommended. Source: https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/instagram-graph-api/reference/ig-user/media, read 2026-11-20.
- Official, quoted: "Trial reels are reels that are only shared to non-followers." `graduation_strategy` `MANUAL` = graduated by hand in the app; `SS_PERFORMANCE` = graduated automatically if the reel performs well. Source: https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/content-publishing/, read 2026-11-20.
- Official limitation: "Shopping tags are not supported." and "Filters are not supported." Same source, read 2026-11-20.
- Verified against the markdown source, quoted in full: "Standard Access is the default access level for all apps and limits the data your app can get. It is intended for apps that will only be used by people who have roles on them, during app development, or for testing your app. If your app only serves your Instagram professional account or an account you manage, Standard Access is all your app needs." Source: https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/instagram-platform/overview.md, read 2026-11-20.
- Official, quoted: "Instagram accounts are limited to 100 API-published posts within a 24-hour moving period. Carousels count as a single post. This limit is enforced on the `POST /<IG_ID>/media_publish` endpoint". Source: https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/instagram-platform/content-publishing.md, read 2026-11-20.

### Facebook Pages video / Reels publishing API

- Official: a dedicated Reels endpoint exists: "This document explains how to publish a reel on a Facebook Page." Steps: POST `/{page-id}/video_reels` with `upload_phase=start`, upload the bytes (or a `file_url` header) to `rupload.facebook.com`, then POST `/{page-id}/video_reels` with `upload_phase=finish`, `video_state=PUBLISHED` (or `DRAFT` / `SCHEDULED`), `description` ("Supports hashtags"), `title`, optional `scheduled_publish_time` (10 minutes to 29 days ahead), `is_ai_generated` ("Set to `true` to disclose that the reel was created or edited using AI. Defaults to `false`."), and a `place` (location Page) header. Source: https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/video-api/guides/reels-publishing.md, read 2026-11-20. Local file upload works, so no public URL is needed.
- Official, quoted: "Reels API is limited to 30 API-published posts within a 24-hour moving period. This limit is enforced on the `POST /{page_id}/video_reels` endpoint". Same source, read 2026-11-20.
- Official: permissions `pages_show_list`, `pages_read_engagement`, `pages_manage_posts`, with "A Page access token requested from your app user who can perform the `CREATE_CONTENT` task on the Page". Same source, read 2026-11-20.
- Official: Reels API video spec: .mp4, 9x16, 1080x1920 recommended (minimum 540x960), 24-60 fps, "Duration | 3 to 90 seconds." (as a Page story, max 60 s), H.264/H.265, AAC 48 kHz stereo. Same source, read 2026-11-20. A film longer than 90 s cannot go up as a Facebook Reel through this API.
- Official: hosted files must be fetchable by Meta's crawler: "The API will now reject files hosted on sites that restrict access via robots.txt. Developers need to ensure that the hosting site allows the \"facebookexternalhit/1.1 ...\" user agent". Same source, read 2026-11-20.
- Official: collaborator invites go to other Facebook Pages only, "10 collaborator invitation per Page per 24 hours". Same source, read 2026-11-20.
- Official, quoted, on access without review: "Apps in Development Mode can request any Permission from any app User who has a Role on the app." and "For Business apps, which do not have app modes, permissions must be approved for Advanced access before they can be granted to your app by an app user without a role on the app itself or a role in a Business that has claimed it." Source: https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/pages-api/overview.md, read 2026-11-20. So the owner, as admin of his own Meta app and of the Monholt Page, can publish Reels to that Page with no App Review.

### Threads API

- Official, quoted: "Threads testers can grant your app these permissions at any time. For app users without a role on your app to grant your app these permissions, each permission must first be approved through the App Review process, and your app must be published." Source: https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/threads/get-started.md, read 2026-11-20. So the owner's own @monholtco Threads profile, added as a Threads Tester, can publish through an unreviewed app.
- Official, quoted: "To test your app with a Threads user, you must first send an invitation to the Threads user's profile and accept the invitation. Invitations can be sent by clicking on the Add People button and selecting Threads Tester in the App Dashboard > App roles > Roles tab." and "Invitations can be accepted by the Threads user in the Website permissions section under Account Settings of the Threads website or mobile app". Same source, read 2026-11-20.
- Official, quoted: "If your app has not been approved for advanced access for the `threads_content_publish` permission, you can only post on behalf of Threads testers on your app." Source: https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/threads/posts/accessibility.md, read 2026-11-20.
- Official, quoted: "Testing is only required if you are submitting your app for App Review; if your app will access data you don't own or manage." Source: https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/threads/get-started/create-an-app.md, read 2026-11-20.
- Official: permissions `threads_basic` ("Required for making any calls to all Threads API endpoints") and `threads_content_publish` ("Required for Threads publishing endpoints only"). Same sources, read 2026-11-20.
- Official, quoted: video posts need `video_url`: "Threads will retrieve your video from the URL provided, so it must be on a public server." Then: "Wait about 30 seconds before publishing a Threads media container to give Threads enough time to fully process the upload." Source: https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/threads/posts.md, read 2026-11-20. Unlike Instagram, no resumable local upload is documented for Threads.
- Official, quoted: "Threads profiles are limited to 250 API-published posts within a rolling 24-hour period." Source: https://developers.facebook.com/docs/threads/overview, read 2026-11-20.
- Official: video spec: MOV or MP4, H264/HEVC, AAC audio, 23-60 fps, max 1920 px wide, 9:16 recommended, "300 seconds (5 minutes) maximum", "1 GB maximum". Text "limited to 500 characters" (emojis count as their UTF-8 bytes). Source: https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/threads/posts.md, read 2026-11-20.
- Official, API side of topics: `topic_tag` parameter, 1-50 characters, no periods or ampersands; "Only one topic tag is allowed per post". The in-text `#tag` method "is not preferred but is kept for backward compatibility." Same source, read 2026-11-20.
- Official, API side of links: `link_attachment` (preview card) "is only available for text-only posts. It does not work with image, video, or carousel posts." and "The number of links is restricted to 5 or fewer" (posts with more fail since December 22, 2025 with `THREADS_API__LINK_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`). Same source, read 2026-11-20.
- Official, API side of AI label: `is_made_with_ai=true` "displays the AI info label, as it does when you select the AI-label option in the Threads composer." "Omitting it or setting it to `false` does not add an AI self-disclosure and does not prevent automatic AI labeling." Same source, read 2026-11-20.
- Official: `alt_text` is supported on video posts ("It only works on image, video, and carousel posts."). Source: https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/threads/posts/accessibility.md, read 2026-11-20. The docs index also lists location tagging, geo-gating, spoilers and share-to-Instagram-Stories for API posts: https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/threads/llms.txt, read 2026-11-20.
