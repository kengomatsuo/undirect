Newspapers - Vellum simulation created in Houdini, rendered in Blender.

Another project from mid 2023, this time exploring vellum. Relatively simple simulation, stacked some newspapers, released them into a velocity field to get caught on a statue. Used wedging to test different seeds and noise amplitudes for the velocity, although they didn't differ too much. Played around with environment and cameras a good bit to get a nice looking sequence of shots.

This project was also featured on @houdini3d '2023 Houdini Student Reel'
