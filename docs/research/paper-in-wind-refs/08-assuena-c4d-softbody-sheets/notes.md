## What makes it read as real
- A clean, readable test of the "wave": the wind reaches sheets in order, so they lift one after another and form a ribbon; that staggered timing reads more natural than all sheets lifting together.
- Each sheet curls up from its windward edge first and lifts like a lip before it leaves the floor; in the air the sheets stay mostly flat with one bend.
- Weak points to avoid: identical sheets, even spacing and a uniform grey stage make it look like a test.
