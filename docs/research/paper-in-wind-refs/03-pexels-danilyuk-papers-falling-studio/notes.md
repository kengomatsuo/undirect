## What makes it read as real
- Closest look to our studio sweep: plain seamless backdrop, a few sheets, a still subject. Shows what loose sheets look like against a flat colour with nothing else moving.
- Fall: sheets come down mostly face-on with a gentle side-slip, rocking a little, then one tips and slides off sideways (the flutter-to-glide change from the physics notes). They do not spin end over end.
- Bend: one soft bow across each sheet, corners slightly lifted; printed charts stay readable on the sheets that face camera.
- Focus and blur: the sheets nearest the lens are huge, out of focus and motion blurred, almost transparent; the sharp ones are behind the subject. That depth spread is what our shot lacks.
- Light: big soft key from the front; a sheet facing the light goes nearly white, one tipped away drops to cool grey; the backdrop shows no hard shadows.
- Count: two to five sheets in frame at any time.
- Camera: locked off, low angle, no move.
