## What makes it read as real
- A stack does not come apart in the air on its own: the ream falls as one brick, turning slowly, and only fans out when it lands. Only the outermost few sheets peel away in flight. For our shot: the stack under the stone should stay a solid block; only the top sheet or two should lift, and only where the gust gets under an edge.
- The few free sheets fall face-on and slide sideways in a long low glide across the floor, far from where the brick landed.
- On landing the sheets fan into a shingled arc, each overlapping the next.
- Camera: handheld phone, top-down three-quarter view, room light.
