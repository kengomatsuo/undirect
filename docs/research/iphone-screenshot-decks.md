# iPhone App Store screenshot decks: 26 apps (ASG94)

Read 2026-11-21 for the new Undirect and Cutling iPhone and iPad sets. Each deck is the US store's
current iPhone set, frames 1-5, fetched from Apple's lookup API (`itunes.apple.com/lookup?id=<ID>&country=us`,
`screenshotUrls`), the same images apps.apple.com shows; the App Store page is
`https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id<ID>`. The stills, one strip per app, are on the owner's Drive at
`gdrive:Research/iphone-screenshot-decks/<app>.jpg`; they stay out of this public repo because they are
other companies' artwork. Text positions are read by eye from the stills, as a share of the frame's
height from the top, so they are approximate.

Why it matters: iPip shows an iPhone shot inside a phone frame whose Dynamic Island covers about
y 89-215 of a 2796-tall shot (measured on ipip.fyi/apps/cutling: screen 247x523 CSS, `object-fit: cover`,
island 82x24 at 11 px from the screen's top), so anything in the top ~8% is hidden there.

## What the 26 show

- **Most top decks put copy in the island's band too.** About 20 of the 25 decks that carry copy start a
  headline, kicker or logo row within the top ~8%. Only a centred cover (Bear, 1Blocker, CARROT frame 3), copy at the foot (Halide, Ivory), or a
  headline at 11-28% (Copilot, Clipt, Structured, Todoist) would survive iPip's frame. So the fix is ours
  to make, and it costs little: Structured and Copilot read as top-anchored with the text at 11-18%.
- **The cover names the product.** Most first frames carry the logo or name, a promise, or awards;
  the strongest pair the icon with a short promise (Bear, Flighty, Grammarly, Opal, Copilot).
- **Two to five words win the search strip.** Gboard, SwiftKey, 1Blocker, Opal and Structured run 2-5
  words per frame; the long-sentence decks (Things, Drafts, Raycast) are harder to read at strip size.
- **One background per set.** Nearly all hold one brand colour or texture across the set (Todoist and
  Craft vary the tint per frame); the rainbow gradients of PastePal look the cheapest.
- **Real UI cut free of the phone reads as premium.** Todoist, Copilot, SwiftKey, Paste and Grammarly
  float UI cards out of or without the device, with deep shadows. That is direction B's move on the Mac.

## What the new sets take

- Headline top at 100 CSS px of 956 (300 of 2868, about 10.5%), between Copilot's 11% and the 6-7% norm:
  near the top, clear of iPip's island band by 85 px. Every card stays 64 CSS px clear of the bottom.
- Frame 1 is a cover: icon, app name, then the promise in 3 lines (Bear, Flighty, Opal).
- Real UI crops floated on the brand wallpaper with a deep shadow, no phone shell (Copilot, Todoist,
  Paste), one wallpaper per app (Gboard, Grammarly).
- 3-7 words per headline, centred (Gboard, SwiftKey, Structured).
- Not taken: award laurels and press logos (neither app has them), rainbow gradients (PastePal), tilted
  devices (they make small text unreadable at strip size).
