## What they share
- One idea, one sentence, usually under 60 characters. The longest ones are the biggest names, who spend the length on client names as proof.
- The name field is the brand alone.
- Three shapes: a plain category phrase (Work & Co, thoughtbot), "we make X" with proof or a twist (Metalab, Iconfactory), or a short line with rhythm (Lickability, Panic).
- None lists platforms ("iPhone, Mac, Android and the web"), none says "other companies", and none puts an X profession category under the bio.
- The bio is the same line on every platform of a studio.

## What was wrong with Monholt's (before)
- X: "We make apps for iPhone, Mac, Android and the web, and build them for other companies." Long, a platform list, two "and"s, and X's profession tag "Software developer/Programmer/Software engineer" under it reads as a freelancer, not a studio.
- Instagram, TikTok, Threads: "Phone and web apps, designed and built for other companies." A fragment; "other companies" is vague.
- Two different lines across platforms.
