### Shape of a post

## 8. SEO and ASO lessons

### App Store

Name "Undirect - Tab & Popup Blocker", subtitle "Stop Safari redirects, ad tabs", keyword "new". The App Store combines words across name, subtitle and keywords. (seo-research.md ASG75; c4ca2fdd 2026-11-20 15:11)
